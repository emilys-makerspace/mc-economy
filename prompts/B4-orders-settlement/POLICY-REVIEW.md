## Approved initial order rules — O01–O10

| ID | Approved policy | Precise boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O01 Order types | Whole-share **protected market** and **limit** orders. No naked sells, automatic FX or implicit borrowing. | Quantity follows approved security/venue whole-unit and settlement-lot rules; odd/fractional or unsupported units reject explicitly. Stops, stop-limits, all-or-none/fill-or-kill, trailing orders, auctions, margin and shorts remain separate later scope. |
| O02 Market protection | Market orders are **immediate-or-cancel (IOC)** with a **1% hard price-protection band** from the reviewed executable best ask for buys or best bid for sells. | Reference quote/rules must still be fresh at submission, with original replay resolved first. Tick-round the bound inward so rounding cannot widen protection. The band constrains execution prices; review separately shows maximum total debit/minimum proceeds including current commission and cash effects. Never widen/reprice consent automatically. A user needing a different bound can submit an explicit limit order. Empty/closed/stale/unsupported books refuse; an admitted IOC can fill partially and cancel the rest. No IOC survives as a resting order. |
| O03 Limit lifetimes | Limit orders support **DAY** (default), **GTC capped at 30 real UTC days**, and **IOC**. | DAY expires at the final regular-session close of its assigned venue trading date. A before-open or intraday-break order belongs to the next eligible interval's trading date; an after-close/weekend/holiday order belongs to the next sourced eligible trading date. A lunch break does not expire that day's order. GTC expires at accepted UTC instant + 30×24h even if the exchange is closed; expiry retains actual due time during chronological catch-up. Limit IOC is open-session-only like market IOC. Unsupported TIF rejects rather than silently changes. |
| O04 Closed sessions | Accept funded DAY/GTC limit orders outside open hours when the next eligible session and relevant calendar coverage are known; reserve funds/shares immediately. Refuse market/IOC while closed. | No fill during lunch/holiday/early-close/unsupported/catching-up intervals. Owners can view/cancel outside sessions under current authority. An unknown next session is CALENDAR_UNAVAILABLE, not a guessed weekday. Orders queued during closure share the same accepted-sequence priority rules. Session opening inclusive/closing exclusive remains T03. |
| O05 Matching | **Price, then time priority** on each listing/venue book, treating eligible player and system orders consistently. Better price first; equal price uses durable server accepted sequence. Execute at the resting order/quote price. | Client timestamps, actor type and GUI speed grant no priority. Separate securities/venues do not form one book merely because names match; B5 owns cross-market routes. A purely technical refresh of unchanged economic quote terms does not jump/reset priority; changed price or increased size is a replacement with new priority. Existing quote revisions still invalidate stale consumption. No auction/batch-clearing mechanism initially. |
| O06 Partial fills and reservations | Allow partial fills by default. Reserve the full requested cash-at-bound plus commission for a buy, or full eligible free quantity for a sell, before accepting. | Insufficient full reservation refuses rather than silently reducing requested quantity or changing currency/source. Each fill consumes only its actual claims, updates cumulative fees/lot basis and leaves a precise remainder. Resting DAY/GTC keeps necessary residual claims; IOC/cancel/expiry releases only unexecuted remainder. Respect minimum settlement lots on each partial fill; no invented counterparty completes the order. |
| O07 Player matching and privacy | Enable player↔player, player↔institution and eligible institution↔institution matching. Public views show **aggregated anonymous book depth**, not owner/account/corporation identity. | Own orders/receipts retain authorized detail and private real counterparty audit is preserved for scoped operator inspection. Block same-beneficial-controller self-trades, including a player versus their owned corporation and corporations sharing the same sole owner; cancel the incoming remainder that would self-match, retaining earlier valid fills. System participants have declared control groups and cannot manufacture wash volume. Don't skip an otherwise next-priority self-order to reach a worse third-party price. Submitted/cancelled/self-blocked work creates no trade volume or P05 pressure. |
| O08 Cancellation, edits and authority | Cancellation races serialize with fills; cancel only the remainder. Initial order edits are **cancel and submit a new order**, with new priority and fresh review. | Resolve original cancellation before a replacement that might double reserve/spend. Completed fills never reverse. Disconnect/closing a menu does not cancel a standing order. Corporate role/integration revocation invalidates remaining delegated order authority: cancel the unfilled remainder before any subsequent fill, preserving prior receipts. Owner may submit a newly reviewed replacement, not silently adopt a revoked actor's consent. Captured commission policy remains per order, but if sponsor/eligibility changes make it invalid, cancel the remainder and require new review instead of silently repricing. Initial bank provider switching already requires no open orders under R02. |
| O09 Notional precision and fees | Retain exact price/tick/quantity calculations. Execute only a legal **whole-unit settlement lot whose price×quantity is exactly representable in settlement-currency minor units**; otherwise refuse that candidate quantity/route explicitly. | B1/B4 freeze compatible venue-lot and settlement-step rules before activation, using exact quote-unit normalization and checked arithmetic. Do not round a sub-minor trade independently on each side or allow order-splitting to create a profitable cash-rounding cycle. No fabricated fractional-cash balance or silent alteration of catalog ticks. The one exact gross notional is identical buyer debit/seller credit before separately posted fees. Current Tier 1 0.25%/qualified Tier 2 zero and cumulative per-order fee rounding remain unchanged; R07 conserved lot-basis allocation and Stage A FX rounding are separate. If approved instrument/whole-share policy conflicts with settlement compatibility, surface that exact instrument constraint rather than silently exclude/change it. |
| O10 Opening/closing and recovery | Revalidate authoritative session, order/claim/quote/source revisions and live financial authority in the fill transaction. Commit cash, shares, liquidity, fees, order/fill facts and original outcome together. | At the exact closing instant no new fill; any earlier committed fill stays. Advance chronological expiry/session transitions before processing affected later work; no client backdating. Repeated original submission/fill/cancel returns original result, changed canonical payload conflicts. Unknown commit gates replacement work until lookup/recovery resolves it. Source/calibration/readiness gaps are unavailability, never permission to mint liquidity or assume a fill. |

## Concrete outcomes

- Buy 10 protected-market units, only 6 executable within 1%: fill 6 and cancel 4; charge commissions only on 6. If admission cannot reserve the full requested bound/fee, refuse before any fill rather than silently submit 6 instead.
- Limit buy 10, fill 4, then cancel: keep 4 acquired units and their actual cost/fees; release only residual cash. A conflicting late fill plan for the released 6 refuses.
- Place DAY during a sourced lunch break: it can execute after the break and expires at that trading date's final close, not immediately at the break. A DAY placed after the final close targets the next sourced trading date; GTC's30-day UTC deadline is distinct.
- A manager's corporate order fills 4 before revocation: those 4 stay committed; revocation prevents subsequent 6 from filling under that authority. A new owner order has fresh review/priority and does not repeat the first 4.
- Sub-minor compatible-lot example: a valid tick quote at 10.5 JPY requires an even number of units for exact whole-yen notional. A 2-unit fill is 21 JPY; a 1-unit cash fill is unsupported under O09. Venue board-lot constraints may require a larger common step. This is an approved policy consequence, not a claim about a specific exchange's actual lot rules.
