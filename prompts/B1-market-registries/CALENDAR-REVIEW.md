## Approved calendar policies — T01–T07

| ID | Approved policy | Consequence / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T01 Trading dates | Observe each venue's actual trading weekdays, official holidays, early closes and scheduled intraday breaks. Use versioned venue-specific reference data, not a single global weekday calendar. | A venue can be closed while another is open. Cross-listing arbitrage must respect each leg's actual session; a calendar closure is not an executable quote. Exact venue calendars must be researched. |
| T02 Session scope | First release executes only within the venue's regular continuous trading intervals. Exclude pre-market, after-hours and simulated opening/closing auctions initially; observe lunch breaks where supplied by the venue rules. | Imported official closing-price seeds may reflect a venue's official closing process; recording a sourced close does not implement an auction. Advanced auction execution requires separate scope. Viewing last prices/positions and authorized cancellation can remain available outside trading intervals. Closed-session order admission/queuing follows separately approved BD05/TIF rules. |
| T03 UTC and DST | Store sourced venue-local rules with IANA zone and retained time-zone/calendar versions, resolve exact UTC intervals, and compare them against the mod UTC clock. Handle DST and special local-date rules in that conversion. | No host-local clock, fixed year-round UTC offset or Minecraft time/sleep controls finance. Opening instant is inclusive and closing instant exclusive. Ambiguous/unresolvable schedule data makes the affected session unavailable rather than producing duplicate intervals or guessed hours. Commit-time session checks resolve close/fill races. |
| T04 Downtime | Preserve existing chronological real-elapsed-time catch-up. Replay retained session transitions and actual pending obligations/orders under captured rules, gradually and without dropping elapsed time. | Affected execution stays catching-up until ready. Closed intervals create no fictitious trades/volume. Offline price-model cadence and autonomous execution depend on approved BD03/B4 policies; a calendar alone does not invent fills or reroll prices. Order expiry must retain its actual deadline. |
| T05 Coverage and updates | Ship locally stored, reviewed calendar coverage for the actual venue/date range supported. Add subsequent official coverage through a reviewed mod/data update or authorized operator import; no live-calendar service required for ordinary runtime. | If a date lacks valid coverage, block affected market execution with a clear diagnostic while Minecraft and independently ready banking continue. Do not guess holidays or assume an exchange is open to extend coverage. Exact available years, source/license and update mechanism are B1-01/02 feasibility work, not already verified facts. |
| T06 Exceptional changes | Support an audited, prospective calendar revision for an extraordinary closure or schedule correction, with effective UTC boundary, reason/source and operator review. | Retain the old versions used by existing receipts/history/catch-up. Never rewrite an executed trade retrospectively. B8 market/risk halts remain distinct from exchange calendars, with the strictest applicable no-trade restriction honored. Automated news-driven real-world closure feeds are excluded initially. |
| T07 Cross-market behavior | B5 checks each eligible route's source/destination sessions, delivery restrictions and executable depth before new legs. Closed destinations do not provide guaranteed offset liquidity. | One leg committed before closure remains committed. Keep actual remaining inventory/exposure and resume/unwind only under approved finite B4 orders and BD11 rules. Reporting-date/seed-close differences are not simultaneous arbitrage quotes. |

## Ownership and future evidence

B1 owns sourced venue identity, zone, calendar versions, covered dates and pure bounded interval lookup. B3 owns persisted session state/readiness and quote invalidation; B4 owns commit-time execution/cancel/expiry ordering; B5 owns route/leg availability; B8 owns its separately approved halt/auction/advanced capabilities. Banking/debt/FX clocks retain their own accepted schedules rather than inheriting an exchange holiday.

A future affected implementation should select meaningful evidence for calendar differences, UTC/date/DST boundaries, early closes/breaks, unsupported coverage, original-version catch-up and a closed second arbitrage leg where needed. These are acceptance concerns, not a mandate for seven tests or an automatic matrix/full-suite run.
