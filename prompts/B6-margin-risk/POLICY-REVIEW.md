One facility per brokerage. Corporate/institutional borrowing and short selling remain unavailable. | Explicit kiosk agreement with the sponsoring lender records collateral, interest/calendar, call/forced-sale and residual-debt consent. Cure requires restoration to the 50% initial requirement and all applicable blockers resolved; current fresh marks can cure but missing data cannot. New orders/withdrawals remain restricted during call/unknown risk. The grace clock runs through downtime/closures; actual sale still requires an open eligible venue. A margin call alone is not a fabricated missed-interest payment/default report. The 30-minute call clock differs from the 20-minute financial interest day. |

Failed/no-liquidity work waits for a bounded retry condition: no more than one new liquidation intent per facility per 60 real seconds, after original-outcome resolution. Closed/halted/unknown venues never produce fake fills. No X08-style 2% loss-consent ceiling applies to mandatory margin liquidation: agreement must disclose that larger losses can be realized. Secured claim/creditor ownership must survive any subsequent collection transfer; no unsecured fallback that drops pledges. |

## Worked risk examples

With four equally weighted same-currency eligible issuers and no haircuts, assets CAD 10,000 and debt CAD 4,000 imply adjusted equity 6,000/10,000=60%, above initial 50%. If assets fall to CAD 6,000 while debt stays 4,000, equity 2,000/6,000≈33.33% falls below maintenance 35%, opening a call. An eligible CAD 1,000 deposit swept to principal makes debt 3,000 against assets 6,000, restoring 50% before any extra fee/interest. A sale may also cure by paying down debt, but only actual net proceeds count.

A foreign-currency holding worth 10,000 contributes 9,000 to A after its 10% FX haircut before any concentration adjustment. This is one asset component, not the complete account risk ratio. If that issuer exceeds 25% of G, further deduct 20% of its excess portion. Include every other eligible asset, own cash, cross-listing aggregation, accrued debt and post-fill fee in the complete equation.

After a call deadline at a closed exchange, the plan remains pending and debt continues under its captured calendar; it cannot sell at yesterday's close or reset the deadline. If 10 offered shares receive 4 actual fills, 6 remain restricted and only the 4-sale net cash reduces debt. Actual losses may exceed any model expectation and a deficiency remains payable under the accepted lifecycle.
