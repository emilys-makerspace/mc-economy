## Confirmed requirements and answers

- Players may merge companies they control, whether both are their own or the counterparty is another player's company. Cross-player mergers require both companies' valid consent, never a unilateral acquisition of another player's assets.
- Player corporations may issue new shares, designate share classes and perform stock splits. Dividends, mergers/delistings and split handling must use the same corporate-action machinery for these issuers and the real-company-inspired catalog, with distinct issuer authority.
- Shares start **private**, with a separate **optional exchange-listing process**. Registration/issuance never automatically creates executable listing or financial marks.
- **Founder controls corporate actions initially**, with an option for that corporation to transition to **voting according to share-class rights**. This distinguishes economic shareholders from administrative/action authority. Manager banking/payment/trading grants do not imply share issuance, class alteration, merger or governance-transition rights.
- The founder→share-class voting transition is **one-way**, accepted 2026-11-16 with “these changes work for me”. Once effective, no return-to-founder-control mode or unilateral founder revocation of voting rights is available.
- **Fictional in-game news**, not live real-world news or a historical-news replay, drives the simulated catalog. News events for player corporations must distinguish actual authorized action announcements from invented external narrative.

These requirements supersede fixed-single-founder economic ownership and no-player-equity/merger exclusions only for the accepted B8 expansion. B0's initial registration remains one founder, with no shares/listing fabricated on registration. Corporate borrowing/shorting/lending, personal guarantees, tax/government and specific integration mods remain excluded unless separately requested.

## Equity and authority handoff

B0 owns stable corporation identity, initial founder and account/membership grants. B1 owns company/security/class/listing references; a B8 player issuer explicitly links the corporation without repointing the original catalog IDs. B2 owns cap-table/custody quantities, subscriptions, lots and transfer restrictions; Stage A owns all actual subscription/dividend/merger cash and FX. B8 owns issuer mandates, governance proposals/approvals, class-right versions and effective corporate actions. B4/B5/B6/B7 remain owners of affected orders/conversions/collateral/returns.

Share classes need explicit immutable-at-issue/versioned terms: quantity unit, authorized/issued/treasury quantities, voting rights, dividend entitlement and liquidation/successor rights, plus any transfer/conversion restrictions. Class changes cannot silently rewrite outstanding holders' agreed rights. Founder-control mode must be clearly disclosed to subscribers, including dilution and merger authority. The [approved equity and action rules](EQUITY-AND-ACTIONS-REVIEW.md) set voting and non-voting ordinary classes with equal economic rights per share, protective class votes and a 24-real-hour pro rata offer for new cash issues.

Issuance creates a balanced security movement with an explicit issuer/treasury counterparty and cap-table revision. A subscription names actual source/destination owners and funds company cash through Stage A in the same accepted cash/share transaction; no equity price mints cash or implies independent net worth. Unsubscribed authorized shares are not spendable cash or a founder holding. Issuance/transfer permission, applicable class rights and original outcome are revalidated at commit. General extension APIs use these mandates rather than an unrestricted mint/merge hook.

Governance transition is an explicit proposal with current controller authority, affected class terms and cap-table/rights versions. The transition is one-way with 24 real hours' notice. The [approved equity and action rules](EQUITY-AND-ACTIONS-REVIEW.md) define the 48-real-hour voting window, routine quorum and majority, major-action threshold and affected-class approval. Original founder identity remains historical provenance, not perpetual authority after the governing mode changes; banking/manager/integration/action mandates must be reconciled against the accepted new governance rather than leave a founder backdoor. Persist founder/voting mode history; do not infer control from a share-price display or a stale original founder grant after transition. Freeze actual control identity for O07 same-controller trade prevention; multi-shareholder economic ownership alone neither grants managers control nor automatically makes all shareholders one controller.

## Dividends, splits and mergers

The proposed shared action lifecycle stages issuer authority, immutable announcement/entitlement inputs, actual funding, approval, effective UTC boundary and bounded atomic publication. Player issuers cannot rewrite the real-company-inspired catalog through an admin owner field. A dividend requires approved class entitlement/record/ex/payment policy and actual corporate funding; no automatic bank debit beyond the exact authorized amount or money issuance. Player corporations reserve the full cash payout before announcing it and give 24 real hours' notice of the shared record and ex-dividend cutoff. B7 dividend equivalents remain separate borrower obligations.

Splits transform issued/treasury/holder quantities, lots with conserved total basis, pending subscription entitlements, orders, classes/ratios and any eligible return/collateral claims. The initial policy uses one exact ratio across the issuer's classes, cancels affected unfilled orders and triggers at effectiveness, and funds exact fractional entitlements under the approved settlement rules. A split creates no economic cash or independent wealth solely from multiplying quantities. Source facts/history remain original; adjusted analytical series are separate.

A merger proposal binds both corporation IDs, approval/control modes, relevant shareholders/classes, surviving or new issuer, exchange ratio, cash consideration, assets, liabilities, accounts, contracts/grants, open orders and integration claims. Same-founder mergers require authorized approval for both distinct corporations; cross-player mergers require matching approvals of the same manifest by both actual authorities, with class votes where voting mode requires them. Neither founder can transfer the other company's bank cash on their own. Changed cap table/terms/assets invalidate stale approval according to the frozen plan contract.

The plan must preserve shareholder and creditor rights through exact successor shares/cash/claims, not delete an absorbed company's debt or gift minority shares to a founder. Resolve corporate four-BANK-account/currency/provider constraints before publication: consolidate or retain explicitly eligible account ownership/closed-history links under approved rules; do not silently turn two four-account companies into an eight-account exception. Old corporation/account/security IDs remain historically resolvable with successor references. Manager/integration rights do not automatically union/escalate in a successor. External obligations without supported successor authority block rather than vanish. The initial policy names one survivor, requires the same approved terms in both companies, and protects affected classes and disinterested shareholders. A cross-player survivor uses share-class voting at closing. A same-owner survivor may retain founder control if both companies were in founder mode.

Delisting removes exchange execution, not the corporation, bank accounts, equity ownership or obligations. A private company may remain after delisting under explicit rights/transfer rules. Any unreturned shares, dividend claims, collateral or in-transit B5 conversion requires source-compatible successor treatment. Corporate dissolution is separate and cannot erase a live cap table or creditor claim.

## News and the UTC question

Current source/contracts use UTC instants (`Clock.systemUTC()` in ordinary server service construction). The financial calendar partitions elapsed UTC into 20-minute days, 28-day months and 364-day years; this is a **calendar scale**, not a second definition of UTC or an automatic time-zone offset. Exchange-local schedules resolve to UTC under T01–T07; different venue-local closing times are not different UTC standards. Historical 2026-11-13 source-price dates remain seed provenance, not the running simulation's news date.

With the approved fictional-news choice, schedule each event on the server economy's authoritative UTC timeline. Retain event ID, target issuer/sector/security, selected result/seed, source kind, effective UTC instant, narrative and factor-policy versions, and commit/outcome identity. A human financial-calendar date may be a display projection; it does not reschedule the event. Never use a live real company's publication date, Minecraft time or a client-local clock to apply it.

**Approved news semantics, accepted 2026-11-16:** apply the first event effect, invalidate/reprice affected quote generations and publish the announcement at one effective model boundary under an atomic generation/gate. Do not reveal a guaranteed numerical future jump while leaving an old executable book open. Apply once per underlying issuer/security factor group, not once per cross-listing; each venue has its own actual session/liquidity/FX and B5 consumes real discrepancies. Events while closed may affect retained fundamental/model inputs, with no closed-session fills; trading at reopening uses current admitted generations. Offline catch-up uses original selected events and cannot reroll or reannounce them as fresh future news.

All fictional news is labeled simulated and uses the in-game issuer identity; no claim that a real source company actually had that event. For player corporations, issuance/dividend/merger/split announcements derive from authorized actual action facts. Free-text posts alone cannot change economic marks, mint cash, declare a funded dividend or merge companies. Random narrative may not secretly dilute a player company, seize assets or execute an action without governance authorization. The approved rules cover synchronized announcement and first effect, once-per-underlying application, original offline-event recovery and fact-backed player-action announcements. Follow the [approved news calibration](NEWS-CALIBRATION-REVIEW.md) and [stop and halt rules](STOPS-AND-HALTS-REVIEW.md).
