# Approved curated issuer actions at launch

The user approved this launch policy on 2026-11-17. Use the shared corporate-action services for curated in-game issuers. Give the server operator an explicit way to schedule those actions for identities the simulation controls. Player-owned issuers use their own founder or shareholder approvals.

| Decision | Approved launch rule |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduling authority | Require an authorized operator to submit a reviewed action plan for a curated issuer. Publish an in-game announcement at least 24 real hours before the entitlement or effective cutoff. The plan captures the affected instruments, terms, funding, UTC times and original operation identity. Simulated earnings and headlines affect reference prices through B8-05. Actions that pay cash or change rights come from the reviewed plan. |
| Dividends | Pay a fixed amount per eligible share from cash the issuer has available and reserves up front. Skip a distribution if the issuer has no eligible funding source. Use the shared custody snapshot and separate borrower-funded dividend equivalents. A simulated earnings surprise alone creates neither cash nor an entitlement. |
| Splits | Support exact forward and reverse ratios across the issuer's classes with one effective boundary across all affected listings. Preserve cost basis, pledged shares and loan-return claims. Cancel and release affected unfilled orders and stops for resubmission. A fractional settlement requires exact claims and independently valued, reserved cash. |
| Mergers | Require a reviewed plan for both curated issuers naming the survivor, exchange ratios, any reserved cash, and treatment of custody, collateral and outstanding share returns. Permit a full cash-out of a holder's stake only with that holder's consent. Defer a deal whose terms cannot preserve the affected rights and obligations. Deals involving player corporations require their approved governance and are outside this curated-issuer scheduling policy. |
| Delistings | Close trading on the affected listing and cancel affected open orders. Keep holdings, obligations and history accessible. Another listing remains eligible only through its own session and custody checks. A terminal payout requires a separate funded action. |

A scheduled split for a cross-listed issuer publishes once on master UTC. The same action changes units on both venues at that boundary, even if one venue is closed. News, quote invalidation and index continuity use the same action record. Each venue resumes execution under its own session and reopening rules.
