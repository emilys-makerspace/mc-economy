# Approved stop orders and trading halts

The user approved these launch rules for B8-01 and B8-02 on 2026-11-17. They build on existing price-time priority, order reserves, real venue sessions and durable settlement. See [the order rules](../B4-orders-settlement/POLICY-REVIEW.md) and [the calendar rules](../B1-market-registries/CALENDAR-REVIEW.md).

| Decision | Approved rule |
| --- | --- |
| Stop trigger | Offer buy and sell stops on a specific listing. A sell stop fires on the first eligible settled trade at or below its stop price. A buy stop fires at or above it. Only regular-session fills committed after the order was armed count. Quotes, model marks, old closes and trades on another listing have no trigger authority. If a listing has yet to trade, its first eligible fill can trigger a standing stop. |
| Protected stop-market | At activation, attempt an immediate-or-cancel order. The owner supplies a minimum sell price or maximum buy price when arming the stop. Review that hard bound and the usual commission before reserving cash or shares. Apply B4's inward-rounded 1% protection from the first current executable quote at activation, subject to the owner's original bound. An open book with insufficient eligible liquidity can produce a partial fill or an unfilled terminal result. Release the unused reservation and show the reason. |
| Stop-limit | At activation, submit one ordinary limit order at the price chosen when the stop was armed. If the book gaps past that price, the order may rest until price returns within the limit, cancellation or its original expiry. The child gets its own price-time priority when admitted and inherits the parent's expiry. Reserve funds or shares when the stop is armed and transfer the existing reservation to the child. |
| Lifetime and ownership | Use DAY by default and up to 30 real days for GTC, with the original calendar deadline applying to both parent and child. Initial scope is cash-funded buys and owned-share sells. A committed trigger records one child intent and the source fill. Cancel or authority revocation can release only unexecuted claims. When an unfilled stop loses corporate delegation, apply the approved revocation rules. |
| Automatic volatility halt | Pause the affected listing for five real minutes when eligible settled prices move at least 10% within a rolling five-minute window, confirmed by two subsequent prints on the same side of the threshold relative to the reference fill. Use the earliest eligible fill in the same continuous session and five-minute window as the reference. The reference and confirming fills must collectively involve at least three distinct beneficial-controller groups, with distinct controller pairs for the two confirming fills. Use exact price comparisons. A sparse book can remain tradable with an explicit insufficient-sample status. After reopening, start a fresh observation window. |
| Manual halt | An authorized operator can declare an audited halt for a specific listing, an underlying across venues, or a venue, with a reason and a defined review or release condition. Corporate founders and managers may request review through the ordinary channel. Halt authority stays with the bounded operator path. Overlapping session, action and halt restrictions all apply. |
| During a halt | Suspend matching and reject new orders and replacements for the affected listing. Allow owners to inspect and cancel. Resting limits keep priority, reservations and original expiry. If a fill triggers a stop and a halt together, journal both and apply the halt before any further execution. Hold the stop's single intent until reopening and recheck its original expiry, authorization and price bounds. Interest, margin calls, recalls and due entitlements continue on master UTC. Forced liquidations and buy-ins wait for eligible execution. |
| Reopening | Resume at the first regular-session instant at or after the halt deadline when book, custody and session state have reconciled and other restrictions have cleared. Publish a fresh book generation and resume ordinary price-time matching. An empty reconciled book can reopen for limit orders. Market execution still depends on current eligible depth. Preserve priority for unchanged resting orders. Process elapsed expiries before matching, and retain the original halt and trigger facts across restart. |

## Examples

- A sell stop at CAD 90 with a limit of CAD 88 fires on a CAD 85 print. The resulting order can sell at CAD 88 or better and can remain open if nobody bids that high. A stop-market with the same hard floor attempts an immediate sale under existing protection and closes any unfilled remainder.
- A fill trips both a stop and a volatility halt. The stop is recorded once. If it expires while trading is halted, its reservation releases at the original deadline. If it is still live at reopening, the same trigger resumes against current execution conditions.
- A halt expires during a venue break. Orders can be cancelled during the break. The book reopens once the regular session resumes and state has reconciled, even if the book is empty.

The five-minute halt and confirmed 10% move within five minutes are approved launch settings. The B8 checkpoint must confirm the eligible fill source, bounded observation window and serialized halt and trigger order. This review leaves later order types and market-wide volatility thresholds for separate policy. See [the B8 queue](EXECUTION.md) and [project verification policy](../../.codex/VERIFICATION.md).
