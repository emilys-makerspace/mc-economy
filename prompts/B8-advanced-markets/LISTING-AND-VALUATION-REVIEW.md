# Approved player-company listing and valuation rules

The user approved the first-release rules for B8-08-04 on 2026-11-17. Private shares may qualify for optional exchange listing under these rules. See [the corporation expansion](CORPORATIONS-EXPANSION.md) and [approved equity and action rules](EQUITY-AND-ACTIONS-REVIEW.md).

| Decision | Approved launch rule |
| --- | --- |
| Venue and capacity | Let the company select one supported existing venue and settle in that venue's supported currency. Use the venue's regular session, tick and lot rules. Allow one publicly listed class per issuer on one venue, with up to 40 active player listings per world. Allocate a slot at activation so concurrent activations respect the cap. Player listings have distinct identities and sit alongside the 800 curated listings. Use 40 as the launch limit and include the combined universe in later workload planning. |
| Approval and disclosure | Require approval from the current founder or, in voting mode, at least two-thirds of all eligible voting power. Apply the existing protective class votes when listing changes affected rights. Require an operator to review an application against published admission rules under a versioned disclosure. Publish the class terms, governance mode and controlling party or group, outstanding shares, public float, settlement currency, and material actions already approved or announced. Keep individual orders and account details private. A changed application requires fresh review. |
| Public float | At admission, require at least 20% of the class's outstanding shares to be settled, unrestricted and held by at least three distinct holder groups outside the issuer's controlling group. Exclude treasury shares from the denominator. Exclude restricted, pledged and loaned shares from the qualifying float. Apply existing control-group rules. Eligible holders may count toward float without putting their shares up for sale. |
| Opening and ordinary trading | Let holders list an existing class without making a new issue. If the issuer raises capital first, preserve the approved 24-real-hour pro rata offer. Open with ordinary prefunded limit orders and settled offers to sell through B4. The first genuine fill involving an independent counterparty establishes the first traded price at the resting order's price. A founder's subscription price, curated-market seed and nominal quote are separate from that price. Admit protected market orders once a genuine first fill exists and valid executable liquidity supports the relevant order protection. An empty or one-sided book remains visible with its actual liquidity status. |
| Who provides liquidity | Use actual, funded bids and shares available to sell from participating brokers and holders. Any later institutional market-making mandate needs its own funding, inventory and risk terms. Listing itself confers no access to the curated pool. Keep anonymous public depth and the existing same-controller protections. Post-admission thin trading alone does not trigger delisting. |
| Portfolio display and financial eligibility | Show last traded price, its timestamp and any executable bid or ask separately. Label stale prices. If there has been no fill, show “No trades yet” and leave market value unavailable. Historical cost remains available. At launch, exclude both private and listed player equity from Tier 2, margin or other collateral, and short lending eligibility. Exclude it as an unsupported asset type rather than reporting a zero valuation or disabling ordinary wealth qualification on otherwise eligible assets. Preserve actual liabilities and eligible assets under their own existing rules. |
| Later independent valuation | Treat financial eligibility as a separate policy decision after observing trading. A candidate method could use repeated settled trades from distinct, unrelated control groups, a minimum depth and volume requirement, and an independently published mark with retained inputs and freshness. Thin, stale or concentrated trading cannot renew that mark on its own. Specify and approve the exact evidence, freshness, haircuts and funding terms before including these shares in financial decisions. |

A company with 10,000 outstanding shares qualifies on float if at least 2,000 eligible shares are spread across at least three outside holder groups. It can open for limit orders before anyone trades. If one share then trades for CAD 500, that price is visible in the trade history. It does not add CAD 5 million to the company's bank balance or create lending capacity for a shareholder.

B8-08-04 must pin custody, controller identity, operator authority, disclosure refresh and first-fill readiness before implementation. A later financial eligibility policy would define the independent mark required for those products. Actual settled order funding and share custody are the prerequisites for the first limit-order match. See [the listing package](B8-08-04.md) and [the verification policy](../../.codex/VERIFICATION.md).
