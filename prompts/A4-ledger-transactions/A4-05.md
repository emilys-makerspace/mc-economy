## Scheduling and configuration

FX uses existing quote freshness after original-operation lookup. Consume quotes atomically with balanced currency legs. Pay fees to the game and round payouts down to whole coins. Funded closure uses the specified distinct open same-owner BANK destination and settles before closing in the same transaction, including zero payout. For rebase, recheck live authority and the full plan, retire collisions before changing primaries, round balances down and preserve raw history. Size the atomic write against journal limits before enabling it.

Limit implementation to this package. Use existing server authority and journal admission. Leave cash items, custody, and external integrations to their owning phases.

## Diagnostics

Distinguish stale or consumed quotes, blocked closure, changed roster or claims, and write-bound or reconciliation failures.
