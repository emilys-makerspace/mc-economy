## Prerequisites

A4-03 and A3 primary, account and funding contracts. See the [journal contract](../../.codex/architecture/journal.md), [accounts contract](../../.codex/architecture/accounts.md), [A2-04 quote handoff](../../.codex/A2/A2-04.md) and [A2-05 denomination handoff](../../.codex/A2/A2-05.md) as applicable.

## Scheduling and configuration

Settle primary payments, owned-account transfers and authorized credit/debit on the worker. Resolve recipient primary and check live authority, revisions and funds inside settlement. Use A4-01 counteraccounts for issuance and redemption.

Limit implementation to this package. Use existing server authority and journal admission. Leave cash items, custody, and external integrations to their owning phases.

## Diagnostics

Distinguish missing primary, stale revision, revoked authority and insufficient funds.

## Foundation acceptance

Verify paired identity, concurrent overspend, primary changes and disconnect after submission. Hand off settled operations to A4-05 and A4-06.

Record the contract, checks run, and any unresolved prerequisite in the handoff. Stop before implementing the next ID.
