## Concrete conservation examples

- A maker beginning with 100 shares reserves 60 in sell quotes; a 25-share fill leaves 75 owned, 35 still reserved and 40 otherwise free. Refresh cannot restore the sold 25.
- Cash seed allocation CAD 1.68T + CAD 840B + CAD 280B = CAD 2.8T, subject to explicit captured-currency conversion/residual facts. Initial stock value is separate game-security issuance, not another CAD 28B cash credit and not a promise of redemption.
- Buy on listing A and sell the same fungible security on B cannot duplicate custody. If only 4 of 10 delivered units sell, 6 remain and both actual cash legs are retained. A closed B venue offers no guaranteed offset.
- An expensive whole unit can exceed a quoted CAD 5,000 cap; the first level then offers zero units rather than inventing fractional player stock or silently increasing the cap. Deeper levels may still be eligible. B1/B3 readiness reports any instrument that cannot be quoted anywhere under the accepted parameters.
