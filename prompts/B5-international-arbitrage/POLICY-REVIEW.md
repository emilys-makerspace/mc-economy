## Worked outcomes

- A same-security transfer of 10 units completes after 30 UTC seconds with no new transfer fee. Two listings show one conserved holding; the units are unavailable for sale during transit. If only 4 units subsequently sell, 6 remain at the destination with their actual basis.
- A distinct-security conversion consumes 6 free source units under ratio 1/2, credits 3 target units after five minutes, transfers exact basis lineage and charges the one reviewed 0.10% conversion fee. Replaying completes nothing twice; cancellation before due releases input/fee holds. An input of 5 is incompatible, not silently rounded.
- A projected 0.60% gross cash advantage with 0.40% captured/planned costs leaves 0.20% net and does not meet the 0.25% automated threshold. A qualifying forecast may still lose money if the destination moves during transit. Actual executed fees and per-currency legs remain auditable.
- A closed destination with no overlapping session cannot supply an executable institutional offset. Explicit inventory transfer can still complete there, with selling waiting for a valid session/authority. Indicative historical closes are never permission to force a fill.
- Remaining units whose estimated executable exit would lose 3% cannot be auto-sold under the 2% budget. The fund pauses new cycles and reports the real position; no guaranteed 2% loss cap, cancellation of past trades, automatic credit or fake offset is claimed.
