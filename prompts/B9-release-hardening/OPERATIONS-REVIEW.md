# Approved server workload and history policy

The user approved these launch targets and retention rules on 2026-11-17. Run one representative workload through the enabled market and finance services in a disposable dedicated world. Use the nominated A10 reference Mac (Apple M5 Pro, 24 GB RAM, 4 GiB maximum server heap). Keep production UTC and the approved 60-second model and five-second open-session quote cadences. See the [A10 reference setup](../../.codex/A10/verification/A10-08.md).

| Decision | Approved launch rule |
| --- | --- |
| Reference workload | Use all 800 curated listings and the 40-listing player-company allowance with 20 synthetic owners. Start with 400 resting orders, 400 positions and 40 active margin or lending obligations across distinct eligible assets and owners. Sample ten real minutes at two requests per second and inject one 20-request burst. Use 40% quote or history reads, 25% funded submissions including crossing orders, 15% cancels, 10% funding or FX, and 10% margin or lending requests. Retain actual settled fill counts separately. Include natural publication and an overlapping venue session. |
| Responsiveness | Target server tick p95 at or below 25 ms and p99 at or below 50 ms. Target p95 at or below 500 ms for market reads and one second for a durable financial confirmation from admission. Drain the burst to its prior queue range within five seconds. Report accepted, refused and failed requests separately with queue and latency samples. Publish a capacity claim only for the measured workload, exact host and actor type. |
| Queue protection | Allow one active public quote subscription per authenticated player session covering up to 50 listings, pages of up to 100 history points, and up to four market mutations in flight per authenticated actor across its owner contexts. Keep the existing worker capacity as a hard bound. At saturation, defer display work and refuse new excess requests before admission. Accept an original-outcome lookup independently of new-order admission to the extent allowed by the existing bounded worker. Admitted financial work keeps its normal resolution and servicing. |
| Offline recovery | Include a seven-real-day offline interval in the workload scenario and target five real minutes after restart to restore the affected market services. Process due windows and actions chronologically against retained versions. Affected trading waits for its inputs to catch up. Record actual elapsed time and readiness. |
| Chart history | Keep minute points online for 14 real days, hourly summaries for 180 real days, and UTC daily summaries for the life of the world. Keep historical source versions, coverage gaps and timestamps in those views. Move older minute data to verifiable offline archives using reviewed manifests and checksums before removing it from the online series. Keep existing archived and raw history recoverable. |
| Financial records | Preserve ledger entries, orders and fills, entitlement and action records, original operation identities and outcomes, and records needed to reconstruct cost basis and open obligations for the life of the world. Display history limits apply to market chart data. |

Record measured host settings, catalog and fixture versions, actual throughput, queue ages and refusals, catch-up duration and reconciliation outcomes. Use this as one scoped B9 workload measurement, with claims limited to the observed sample. Authenticated external clients are a separate manual evidence scope.
