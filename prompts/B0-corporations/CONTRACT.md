## Scope and dependencies

The user requests player-created corporations/companies, corporate accounts and a supported extension API. Follow-up answers approve one owner plus appointed managers and accounts/payments/transfers first, with corporate borrowing later. **B0 is the corporation foundation package group**, independently deliverable after the current Stage A foundation and before corporate B2/B4 access. It does not require an 800-row securities catalog. Registration creates a stable organization party; a listed issuer remains a separate B1 company/security identity. Player-company stock issuance/listing, multiple equity owners, dividends to owners, tax, government, payroll, succession, corporate credit and specific mod adapters are excluded unless separately selected.

## Domain contract and examples

Corporation record: world-scoped immutable organization ID, normalized unique-name key and display name, owner player UUID, status and revision. Lifecycle DRAFT request → ACTIVE → DISSOLUTION_PENDING → DISSOLVED; tombstoned IDs/names/history never repoint to another owner. Registration, its fee payment, owner assignment and outcome share one journal transaction. No capital or bank account is fabricated on registration. Registration costs CAD 500 or equivalent and the number of corporations per player is unlimited, per explicit follow-up. Under C03 the founder selects an owned open personal bank account with sufficient available cash and reviews the CAD 500-equivalent debit/captured Stage A FX; no borrowing, automatic account substitution or cash-item registration path. The fee goes to a dedicated nonspendable registration-fee sink with journaled retirement. Under C04 names are unique world-wide and case-insensitive after consistent Unicode normalization, 3–48 Unicode code points, using letters/numbers/spaces and ordinary punctuation with no formatting/control codes or reserved bank/system impersonation. Owner-only renaming is free; old names remain reserved to that corporation even after dissolution. Bank debit, fee destination, corporation creation and original outcome share one existing transaction; a rejected or duplicate registration cannot charge twice. Use bounded indexed pages, not a hidden company-count cap.

Membership is a separate revisioned authority record: actor, organization, exact capability set, optional account/spend scope and active/revoked state. Only the owner appoints/revokes managers initially. Managers cannot grant themselves rights, transfer ownership or dissolve the company. Under C01 the owner chooses view/history, payments/transfers and bank-account management separately; new managers start view-only. Payment-enabled managers may spend available company funds, including payments to themselves, as disclosed when granting permission. No custom spending ceilings, co-signing or subdelegation initially. Only the owner appoints/revokes managers, approves integrations and dissolves the company. Under C05 the founding owner remains fixed; transfer, succession and joint equity are deferred. Offline owner presence does not transfer authority. No blanket integration/admin spending power.

Corporate money belongs to the organization, while each journal action retains its real player/integration actor. Under C06 a contribution/distribution is an explicitly authorized transfer between owners with both account proofs, not a balance relabel. Personal-to-company contributions and authorized company-to-player/company payments are supported; they create no equity, dividend, wage or personal-loan product. Corporate payments use Stage A balance/FX/posting services under organization authority. Corporate overdraft, loan/credit issuance and personal guarantees remain unavailable; signed bank balances must be handled explicitly rather than treated as available credit. Under C07 only the owner starts wind-down: stop new accounts/exposure and revoke integrations, while allowing authorized closure/transfers to settle existing funds. Final dissolution requires zero balances, all bank accounts closed and no outstanding obligations/holds or uncertain work. The owner can cancel wind-down before final dissolution; revoked integration permissions do not silently return. No automatic distribution, forfeiture, personal debt transfer or deletion of account/journal history.

Corporate account limits are enforced by organization owner in the same account-opening transaction, independent of the acting owner/manager’s personal account count. Concurrent opening cannot exceed four accounts for that corporation. Corporate bank lifecycle needs an explicit extension to current bank-native/branch admission and eligibility. Approved corporate roles use a company selector at an actual admitted existing bank kiosk with a visible company context; no personal protected-primary auto-creation, automatic Tier 2 qualification, issued card or wealth-source registration. The four-account BANK limit per corporation is approved, separate from personal accounts, following each bank’s existing currency rules. C02 limits initial corporate banking to ordinary products at Tier 1 and hybrid banks. No corporate Tier 2 wealth qualification, deposit yield, issued cards, credit/overdraft or personal guarantees. The owner’s personal wealth/score/credit does not establish corporate eligibility. B0-01 inventories applicable ordinary fees and presents them before implementation; any actual policy gap returns for a specific decision rather than silently inventing a rate. Closing with funds requires an explicit authorized destination and debt/hold checks. Bank/organization/payment changes are validated in the same existing transaction, with stale authority refusal and original-operation recovery.

Under C08 the extension API exposes immutable versioned corporation/account/membership results, authenticated capability-scoped commands and bounded pages. World/session invalidation, owner-thread admission, completion stages, commit-time permission checks and publication audience follow Stage A. Integration identity is server-registered/namespaced with declared capabilities and current organization authorization, never a client-supplied mod ID; idempotent external mappings bind world+integration+external key to one organization without granting spending. API-created player corporations use the same incorporation fee and explicit owner/funding consent; registering an integration is not fee-free corporation creation. Registration does not make arbitrary in-process mod code a security sandbox. No SQL/connection/worker callbacks, synthetic player impersonation or unrestricted grant factory. Revocation/disable blocks new effects; original receipts remain with their actual owner/history. Optional event notifications, if needed for a concrete consumer, are postcommit, bounded and replayable with sequence/deduplication; they cannot authorize a financial effect.
