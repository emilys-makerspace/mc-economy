# Corporation policy review

Prepared and approved 2026-11-13. The user accepted this initial corporation policy set with “this works” after reviewing the recommendations. C01–C08 below are approved design policies; no implementation package is selected, implemented or verified. Read [settled scope and contract](CONTRACT.md), [package queue](EXECUTION.md) and [BD10](../planning/B-DECISIONS.md).

Already settled: one owner plus appointed managers; CAD 500-equivalent incorporation; unlimited corporations per player; four BANK accounts per corporation independent of personal accounts and following existing bank currency rules; company selection at existing bank kiosks; corporate accounts/payments/transfers before corporate credit; extension API for other mods.

## Approved initial policies — C01–C08

| ID | Approved policy | Consequence / deferred alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01 Manager permissions | Owner chooses view/history, payments/transfers and bank-account management separately for each manager. New managers start view-only. Only the owner appoints/revokes managers, authorizes integrations or dissolves the company. Payment permission includes sending company money to other owners, including the manager; disclose this when granting it. | No subdelegation or custom spending ceilings initially. A payment-enabled manager may spend available company funds. Per-payment/daily limits or owner co-signing can be added if wanted, but need explicit currency/time/counter rules. |
| C02 Bank eligibility/products | Ordinary corporate banking at Tier 1 and hybrid banks, under existing native-currency rules and four-account cap. No corporate credit/overdraft, personal guarantees, Tier 2 wealth qualification, deposit yield or issued corporate cards initially. Existing kiosk company context supplies corporate access. | Companies can hold, receive and transfer money without copying the owner's personal score, wealth tier or credit permissions. Tier 2/yield/cards need a separately defined corporate policy; existing personal products remain unchanged. Exact applicable ordinary fees must be inventoried and shown before implementation, with any policy gap returned for decision. |
| C03 Incorporation payment | Founder selects an owned open personal bank account with sufficient available cash. Review the CAD 500-equivalent debit and captured Stage A FX when needed; debit, explicitly recorded registration-fee destination and company creation commit together. No borrowing, automatic account substitution or cash-item payment path initially. | No account is created or charged on failure. A duplicate returns the original company/receipt. Use a dedicated nonspendable registration-fee sink with journaled retirement. A funded recipient treasury is not selected; it would require a separate owner and permitted-use policy. |
| C04 Names | Unique world-wide company name, case-insensitive after consistent Unicode normalization; 3–48 Unicode code points, letters/numbers/spaces and ordinary punctuation, no formatting/control codes or impersonation of reserved system/bank names. IDs remain the authority. | Renaming is owner-only and free; old names remain reserved to the same corporation for history and impersonation protection. Dissolution does not release a name to a different company. Reuse is an alternative requiring alias/disambiguation rules. |
| C05 Ownership | Keep one fixed founding owner for the first release; appointed managers never inherit ownership because the owner is offline. Ownership transfer, succession and joint equity are deferred. | Prevents accidental authority or liability transfer. An explicit owner-to-owner transfer with recipient acceptance can instead be included if desired, as a separately specified lifecycle operation. |
| C06 Contributions/distributions | Permit explicit personal-to-company contributions and authorized company-to-player/company payments through existing services. Record actual actor, source/destination owners, amounts, currencies and fees. Do not represent these as new equity, dividends, wages or personal loans. | No automatic owner withdrawal or private-account payment is implied by owning a company. Company payment rights are required. Multiple owners/equity accounting remain later scope. |
| C07 Dissolution | Owner starts wind-down; stop new accounts/exposure and revoke integrations, while allowing authorized closure/transfers to settle existing funds. Final dissolution requires zero balances, all bank accounts closed, no outstanding obligations/holds or uncertain operations. Retain company/account/history IDs. Owner may cancel wind-down before final dissolution. | No automatic distribution, deletion, forfeiture or implicit transfer of company funds/debt to the owner. Unresolved obligations block final dissolution. |
| C08 Extension authorization | Provide versioned query/registration/account/payment APIs. Other mods acting for a player corporation require explicit owner approval of named capabilities and account scopes. Owner revocation blocks uncommitted work; history keeps the real integration/player actor. API registration follows the same incorporation fee and founder/funding consent. | No universal spending grant, guessed-owner impersonation or bundled specific adapters. A concrete consumer may justify a bounded postcommit notification API; a general event bus is not initially required. Arbitrary in-process Java mods are not sandboxed by this API. |

## Engineering obligations

At B0-01, pin actual interfaces and transaction limits, corporate account admission, fee conversion/rounding and sink posting support, ordinary fee eligibility, role-revision checking, namespace validation and explicit diagnostic codes. Missing technical support is recorded, not treated as an approved workaround. Corporate balances and delegated rights must not inherit personal-only onboarding/primary assumptions. Company count stays unlimited while reads/work remain bounded.

Approval of policies is separate from selecting a package. These accepted choices are reflected in the B0 contract and individual prompts. B0-01 is the next bounded source/authority checkpoint for explicit selection; do not automatically implement its dependents.
