## Outcome

Implement detached supported recipe/tag expansion, exact per-output costs, lowest fully known supported acyclic paths, deterministic ties, bounded provenance and snapshot-scoped caching. Respect frozen cycle/memoization semantics and work/depth/overflow limits. A limit returns unavailable, not a search-order-dependent partial cheapest result. Never invoke arbitrary recipe assembly to infer costs.

Apply the explicit canonical stick rule before alternate recipe search: two oak planks per four sticks, independent of rarer-plank or bamboo crafting origin. Higher species log prices propagate into that species' planks/products, but not generic stick or tool-handle reference values. Preserve actual crafting and consumption semantics.

Apply the approved 75% raw/refined-cooked calibration once to frozen one-to-one processing identities. Refined copper/iron/gold remain CAD 3/6/24; listed cooked meats/fish use revised per-species raw values /0.75. Other supported cooking excludes fuel/time/XP unless its frozen rule explicitly adds processing value. Reversible recipes cannot discount configured anchors or compound processing uplifts downstream.

Do not execute smithing, returned-container/byproduct or unsupported dynamic recipes. Reviewed acquisition anchors can value obtainable outputs without executing those recipes. Unregistered mod outputs are not automatically derived. No brewing, variant algorithms, public SPI or trader activation.

## Acceptance and handoff

A small meaningful fixture may cover multi-output, an anchored reversible cycle and snapshot invalidation/unsupported input. Reuse unchanged exact money/FX evidence. Follow [verification scope](../../.codex/VERIFICATION.md): at most one useful focused named-test invocation and ordinary runnable delivery commands once; no full-suite gate.

Publish `.codex/A9-03-01.md`, current graph/processing architecture and scoped evidence. Preserve pinned versions, registered development mod and working client/server tasks. Stop before A9-03-02.
