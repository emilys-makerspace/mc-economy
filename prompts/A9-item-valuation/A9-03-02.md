# A9-03-02 — Potion brewing derivation

Approved 2026-11-07; requires explicit selection and accepted A9-03-01. Read [CONTRACT](CONTRACT.md), [technical contracts](../../.codex/architecture/a9-shared-contracts.md), [catalog](../../.codex/architecture/a9-item-catalog.md) and A9-01's pinned brewing source map.

## Outcome

Implement the frozen brewing adapter: supported base/effect, strength, duration, splash and lingering transitions with exact identity and recipe provenance. Derive from intermediate potion costs, ingredients, bottle and allocated blaze-powder fuel. Water is explicitly free. Allocate shared ingredients across standard three-potion outputs and fuel across supported brews; no labour/time fee and no dependence on a player's partial batch.

Pinned source supplies 20 operations per blaze powder, so each per-bottle step is prior potion cost plus ingredient/3 plus blaze-powder/60. `PotionBrewing.getRecipes()` does not enumerate vanilla mix lists; use the frozen narrow pinned accessor and capture its version. Dragon breath's returned bottle is recorded with no salvage credit, not counted as another potion bottle; this brewing-specific rule does not enable ordinary crafting remainders.

Count bottle once. Preserve exact internal costs and use published HALF_UP unit values with the one-minor-unit floor at the frozen boundary. Choose cheapest fully known supported acyclic brewing paths; do not invent effect premiums. Reuse graph snapshot/budget semantics. Unsupported custom brewing requires explicit registration/adapter, not callbacks or guessed values.

No generic variant/container recursion, public integration surface, trader switch or activation. Existing filled-potion total is bottle plus brewing-derived contents, not another bottle added afterward.

## Acceptance and handoff

Use a shared-batch numerical example following a transformed potion through captured source/FX and rounding. Confirm bottle/fuel allocation without duplicating every potion permutation. Reuse upstream arithmetic evidence. Follow [verification scope](../../.codex/VERIFICATION.md): at most one useful focused named-test invocation and ordinary runnable delivery commands once; no full-suite gate.

Publish `.codex/A9-03-02.md`, brewing architecture and scoped evidence/coverage limits. Preserve pinned versions and working registered client/server runs. Stop before A9-03-03.
