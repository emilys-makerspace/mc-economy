## Worked accounting example

Acquire 10 shares for EUR 100 plus EUR 1 commission: lot basis EUR 101. Acquire 5 for EUR 60 plus EUR 0.60 commission: second basis EUR 60.60. FIFO sell 12 consumes EUR 101 from the first and EUR 24.24 from the second, for EUR 125.24 basis. Net sale proceeds EUR 150 imply EUR 24.76 realized trading P&L in that currency before any separately reported reporting-currency FX effect. These amounts demonstrate basis conservation; the illustrative fees are not a replacement for the existing commission schedule.

For an indivisible lot basis of 100 minor units over 3 shares, cumulative basis allocations are 33,66,100; individual disposals consume 33,33,34, not a repeatedly rounded per-share amount that loses a minor unit. Partial-fill commissions come from the existing cumulative order-fee accumulator and retain original policy/owner/provider references. No basis projection changes cash balances.

## Feasibility and remaining product decisions

B2-03 freezes immutable lot/FX/reporting proof and residual types; B2-04 composes the authoritative money/custody state atomically; B2-05 financial qualification needs accepted P06 independent fresh marks and complete liabilities.
