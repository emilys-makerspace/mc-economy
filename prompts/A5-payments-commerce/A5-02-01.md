## Scope

Implement versioned custody facts, reservation and save witnesses, committed delivery intent, acknowledgment, startup gating and bounded reconciliation. Preserve original operation IDs. Known undelivered items can become owner-bound durable claims. Authorized operators may close confirmed delivery or redeliver/compensate confirmed loss with evidence and reason. Quarantine uncertainty. Define claim lookup, retention, collection and capacity behavior. Exclude live terminal integration.

Use server-side admission, world-scoped persistence, bounded worker jobs and
server-thread Minecraft callbacks where applicable. Preserve canonical intent
and original operation IDs across retry. Keep the existing ledger and account
contracts available to callers.
