## Contracts and outstanding decisions

- **A2 currencies and FX.** Use stable world-scoped identities, registered historical
  record types and bounded versioned UTC inputs for later economic systems. A2 owns currency
  representation, precision/rounding, FX quote semantics and reproducible rate rules.
  See [storage](../architecture/storage.md) and [clock](../architecture/recovery-clock.md).
- **A3 accounts and identity.** Bind the authenticated player to a server session, resolve
  current authority for a world-scoped account, and re-resolve live targets before delivery.
  A3 owns account identity and actor/delegation authorization rules.
  See [lifecycle and request boundaries](../architecture/lifecycle.md).
- **A4 ledger and transactions.** Commit immutable financial facts, projections and the
  original-ID outcome in one transaction. Use checked arithmetic, balance postings by currency,
  and replay facts through versioned reducers. A4 owns chart of accounts, posting invariants,
  balances/transfers/holds and authorized financial history. See [journal](../architecture/journal.md).
- **A5 payments and commerce.** Preserve original operation IDs across inventory/world save
  boundaries. A5 owns cash/item identities, durable reservation and delivery evidence,
  ambiguous-state quarantine, reconciliation and compensation. See the
  [cross-save contract](../architecture/recovery-clock.md#a5-cashshop-crash-boundary-contracts-only).

The runtime ships without gameplay economic systems. Offline whole-world backup/restore is
supported for clean schema-4 images. MacOS fixtures establish software recovery behavior.
Integrated-world play, arbitrary hardware faults, and market-scale catch-up are outside the evidence.
