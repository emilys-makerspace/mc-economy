# A3-02 account catalog and products

Verified. Schema V6 adds the per-world player account catalog. The trusted
`ServerServices.initializePrimary` entry point uses the committed denomination
read inside the journal transaction. Before selection it returns the durable
`DENOMINATION_PENDING` outcome. After selection use a new operation ID for a
new attempt. Competing accepted first uses resolve to one permanent account ID.

`initializeTrading` provisions a separate, currency-free permanent funding
identity when called by an authorized trading integration. It can be
initialized independently of denomination selection. B2 owns its currency
policy. A3-03 owns ordinary opening and closure, checks against the cap and
currency uniqueness, and current-denomination protection. Use a precheck in
the journal transaction for business rejections, with SQLite constraints as
the backstop. A5 owns the player-facing banking trigger.

A4 must check the recipient primary in the settlement transaction and reject
an incoming payment when it is absent. Create no account and move no funds.
After authorized banking initializes it, a new payment intent may proceed
subject to ordinary checks. A4 also owns the future durable denomination
change, after which the catalog initializer must read that authoritative value.
