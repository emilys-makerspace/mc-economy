# A3-05 account rebase mapping

Verified. The read-only planner consumes proof of a successful A2 second
confirmation and reads the denomination, account versions and claim state in
one worker snapshot. Each primary survives with its ID. A target-currency
BANK collision is marked for retirement with that primary as its successor.
Other accounts remain outside the mapping.

The planner caps one request at 256 primaries. It rejects an oversized roster
before probing claims. Unknown claim state or an affected active hold,
restriction or obligation blocks the whole plan. A repeated authorized plan
is equal while its inputs are stable.

A4 must provide authoritative denomination/revision and claim readers, verify
live authority, rerun the planner in its write transaction, and compare the
complete plan before applying effects. Commit postings, successor links,
account revisions, and denomination together. Establish a separate atomic
write bound based on journal and measured ledger costs. A5 should keep using
stable primary IDs after rebase and resolve closed IDs through the eventual
successor contract. The connected-client chooser acceptance remains a separate
A2-05 item.
