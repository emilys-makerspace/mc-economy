# A3-03 account lifecycle and limits

Verified. Trusted journal registrations open foreign BANK accounts and close
eligible empty BANK accounts. Opening checks the expected primary revision,
currency uniqueness, and the three-account cap in the serialized transaction.
Closure checks source revision and protection, authoritative claims, and the
revision of any supplied destination. A closed account keeps its ID, owner
and currency, with an advanced revision and an immutable journal record.

`AccountLifecycle.eligibility` is the worker-side contract for A3-04 and A4.
Missing ledger state fails closed. A4 must supply a reader over its own
transaction, recheck eligibility there, and commit funded settlement with
the account transition. A5 must authenticate the physical teller interaction
before binding either lifecycle registration. The first banking interaction
still uses A3-02 primary initialization.
