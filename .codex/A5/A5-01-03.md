# A5-01-03 · Payment foundation acceptance

Controlled-clock fixtures exercise the payment registrations and diagnostic
API against disposable real SQLite world stores. Acceptance verifies capture,
cancellation and expiry ordering, one effective terminal transition, conserved
balances, lost-response replay after restart, original receipt identity,
receipt privacy, receipt-write rollback and retryable refund funding.

A 33-invoice expiry backlog releases in batches of 16, 16 and 1. A subsequent
empty batch preserves funds. Existing deadline and backward-clock fixtures,
live-authority checks and diagnostics pagination remain covered.
