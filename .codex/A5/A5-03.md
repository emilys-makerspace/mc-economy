# A5-03 · Cash and cards

Restored all 118 cash and eight single-stack card identities, twelve tabs,
both Canadian bill artworks, original models/textures and localization.
The main Economy tab currently shows a cash/card overview pending terminals.
Collectibles retain their recipe-free behavior. Generic card access grants
no credit facility.

Schema V13 adds world-bound issuance lots with finite remaining quantities.
Persistent stack components survive vanilla splits, merges and actual player
save/restart. Trusted journal APIs compose quantity changes with financial
postings and require authenticated authority plus custody evidence.

Identical copies share their original lot's remaining quantity. The protocol
rejects aggregate overdraw and exhausted lots, while individual identical
copies cannot be distinguished. Restored outstanding lots are quarantined
before readiness until explicitly reconciled with verified evidence.
