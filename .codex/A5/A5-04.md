# A5-04 · Terminal policy

Delivered schema V14 terminal settings and permanent placement-generation
history. Journal-backed PLACE, CONFIGURE and REMOVE enforce live trusted
admission, owner/operator permissions, exact prior generation/revision,
bounded settings and denomination compatibility.

New and replacement terminals start disabled. ATM whitelists accept bills;
tellers accept bills and coins; registers keep cash modes disabled.
Revisions remain monotonic per anchor across replacement. Tombstones and used
generation IDs prevent stale sessions or placements from becoming valid again.

LedgerService exposes the trusted policy journal and owner/operator settings
inspection. Immutable snapshots and the active generation/revision guard are
available to later settlement and geometry adapters.
