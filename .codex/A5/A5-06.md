# A5-06 · Withdrawals

Delivered schema V16 frozen withdrawal intents, bounded exact planning and
atomic bank debit/cash issuance/custody delivery. ATMs use exact bills.
Tellers maximize bill value, minimize bill count and derive coins.
Bill edits revalidate against enabled identities and cannot supply coins.

Confirmation and final admission require complete inventory fit. Space loss
before admission leaves account and issuance unchanged. Known postcommit
non-delivery uses the existing owner claim protocol; uncertainty quarantines.
Actual server inventory delivery is full-batch and uses saved attempt markers.
