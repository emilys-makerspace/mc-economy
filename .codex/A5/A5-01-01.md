# A5-01-01 · Merchant payment foundation

Schema V11 adds world-local merchant grants, invoices, receipts, an expiry
clock and canonical intent bindings for refunds awaiting funds. Trusted
integrations use `LedgerService.merchantPayments` with authenticated
server-thread admission and live worker authority. Receipt reads use a
separate authority check. Integration policies own account control and
operator privileges.

Invoices freeze payer, accounts, currency and amount. Authorization places
one hold for five minutes. Capture, cancellation and expiry consume that
authorization. Capture posts the full amount and writes the receipt in the
same transaction as the invoice and hold transitions. A full refund reverses
the original transfer, writes its own receipt and can retry under the original
operation ID if the merchant has insufficient available funds.

Payment time uses UTC wall milliseconds clamped to a persisted high-water
mark. Downtime counts when wall time advances. A backward adjustment pauses
advancement until wall time catches up. Capture rejects at the exact deadline
and persists that observation before a release is required. A server worker
job runs at most once per second and releases up to 16 overdue holds per pass.
