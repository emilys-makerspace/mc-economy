# A5-02-01 · Custody foundation

Delivered schema V12, versioned journal custody facts, frozen financial intent,
save evidence, delivery attempts and acknowledgment, durable owner claims,
capacity admission and evidence-bound operator resolution. Startup reconciles
in batches of 16 and quarantines restored cases before READY. Compensation
identity survives quarantine and prevents repeated value replacement.

[Current custody contract](../architecture/custody.md) ·
[Verification](verification/A5-02-01.md)

Claim pages and exact case lookup are available through LedgerService.
Claims persist indefinitely and require full-batch capacity for collection.
Adapters must apply attempt markers idempotently and supply verified detached
evidence. Acknowledgment alone cannot close a delivery.

Follow-up verification exercised actual player save/load across server
processes and real container serialization paired with SQLite custody
snapshots, including both skew directions. The evidence records the exact
persistence paths exercised. Restored cases use
conservative operator review. No terminal or physical delivery integration
is enabled here.
