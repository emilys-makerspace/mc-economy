# A5-02-03 · Custody foundation acceptance

Acceptance passed against real SQLite stores, controlled child-process
interruptions and disposable Minecraft server save/restart fixtures.

Ten forced child exits cover before/after reservation save, financial commit,
physical-effect marker, acknowledgment and inventory save. Recovery preserves
original operation identity, settles once, conserves balances and quarantines
uncertain cases without a collectible claim or speculative delivery.

Minecraft server fixtures verify actual player save/load across processes and
actual chest chunk-region save/reload, including count, custom components and
custody markers. A known unavailable chunk remains unloaded on inspection.
Moved items remain present in a second known chest even while the original
chest retains its marker, demonstrating why the marker alone cannot prove loss.

Paired and both skewed SQLite/container restores pass through the opt-in
server fixture. Verified undelivered inventory can become an owner claim;
moved or unavailable observations cannot authorize one. Earlier compensation,
capacity, privacy and bounded startup checks remain covered by the full build.
