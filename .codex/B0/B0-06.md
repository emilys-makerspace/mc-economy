# B0-06 · Native corporate workflows

Founders register companies, name and remove managers, change permissions, rename companies and manage wind-down through player commands. Registration displays the funding account and exact incorporation debit. Approval binds a short-lived review to the connected player. The founder can look up an original receipt after reconnecting. [Player guide](../../README.md#run-a-company)

Existing Tier 1 and hybrid branch kiosks offer company selection, account opening, contributions, payments, transfers and closure. Reviews identify the company, source and recipient, amounts, and any fee or FX publication. An account statement shows its eight most recent postings under current history rights. Kiosk submission captures the original operation for actor-bound lookup. Company and membership revisions are checked at the financial operation. [Server workflow](../../src/main/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/foundation/CorporateKiosk.java)

Banking menu wire 15 requires matching client and server versions. World schema 59, corporation API 1 and BankingIntegration 3 apply. Downstream mods may request founder-approved bank account reads and payments. Future brokerage, trading, ownership and credit capabilities require their own package and authority. [API guide](../../docs/INTEGRATION_API.md#corporation-api-version-1)
