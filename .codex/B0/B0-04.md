# B0-04 · Corporate banking and atomic payments

Schema 58 and banking wire 14 add company selection at a bound Tier 1 or hybrid kiosk. The selector pages founder and active manager memberships, checks authority and displays permitted account balances at the selected bank. Personal and corporate account selections have independent state. The server service supports opening, closing, contributions, payments and transfers. B0-06 owns the native transaction forms. Physical cash continues through Stage A custody.

The [kiosk service](../../src/main/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/foundation/CorporateBanking.java) binds a review to the connected actor, world, selected organization, account/provider revisions and one short-lived kiosk lease. The worker rechecks bank and authority before a fresh effect, then consumes the lease in the journal transaction. Equivalent submissions use the journal's original outcome. Callers retain the operation ID and canonical request to look up status after reconnect. Receipts expose the operation status and code. Reading a balance requires current view rights at the kiosk.

[Corporate accounts](../../src/main/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/corporation/CorporateAccounts.java) use the typed organization owner key and four open BANK slots with one account per native currency. Opening creates a dissolution claim. Closure validates holds, claims, provider and sufficient settlement, checks that the ledger is clear, closes the account and releases the claim in the same commit. Retired identities remain queryable.

[Settlement](../../src/main/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/corporation/CorporatePayments.java) records the acting player and both endpoint owners. The selected personal source or authorized company source must cover principal and fee from available funds. The corporate branch supplies Tier 1 FX at 0.50% of source principal rounded up to a minor unit. The payment fact retains the publication, both currencies, amounts and fee. A legacy unassigned personal account requires bank assignment. Personal yield, credit, overdraft and generic payment paths retain player-only admission.

## Verification

The [SQLite regression](../../src/test/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/corporation/CorporateBankingTest.java) passed on Java 21 after fixes to branch-action dispatch and the fixture's canonical membership request. It exercised four openings and refusal of a fifth, a CAD 100 personal contribution, refusal of a view-only manager payment, CAD 50 converted to USD 35.81 with a CAD 0.25 fee credited to the bank, funded closure, replay and reuse of the closed slot. The fixture used trusted branch admission at the domain boundary. The live multiplayer kiosk, an actual concurrent race and crash recovery were outside this run.

The package build passed after applying repository Java formatting. Gradle resolved runClient and completed its dry run. B0-05 awaits separate selection.
