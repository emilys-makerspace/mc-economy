# B0-03 · Manager capabilities and delegated authorization

Schema 57 adds revisioned, world-local corporation memberships. The [founder service](../../src/main/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/foundation/CorporationService.java) exposes appointment, changes, revocation, original-operation lookup and a founder-only roster of up to 63 rows per page. It authenticates the connected founder again before returning private results. A membership change commits the new rights, immutable audit fact and receipt together. Revoked rows retain their revision across reopen and reappointment.

New appointments are view-only. A founder can independently assign history, payments and account management across all company BANK accounts or a selected set. Granting payment requires acknowledgement that managers can pay themselves. Selected accounts must resolve to the company. Opening a new account requires company-wide management scope. During wind-down the founder can revoke managers and the guard refuses new account opening.

[Managers.guarded and Managers.permits](../../src/main/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/corporation/Managers.java) are worker-side authorization for future corporate banking handlers. Bind the required action and actual affected account to the canonical request and compare both company and membership revisions inside the effect transaction. Multi-account workflows must check each account for which delegated authority is required. The journal resolves an equivalent retry first. Receipt recovery establishes the original effect. Before publishing private financial detail to a manager, B0-04 must separately verify current history rights, account scope and live audience. This package exposes membership detail only to the fixed founder.

The [focused SQLite test](../../src/test/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/corporation/ManagersTest.java) passed with a fixture corporate account and real settlement. It covered account scope, denied self-escalation, payment without history, a self-payment committed before revocation, rejection of a prepared second payment, replay without a second debit, founder-only roster and retained revocation after reopen. This exercised journal ordering on the worker. Live menus and an actual multiplayer scheduling race were outside the test.

Java 21 package build passed after correcting migration formatting. Gradle resolved runClient and its dry run passed. B0-04 awaits explicit selection.
