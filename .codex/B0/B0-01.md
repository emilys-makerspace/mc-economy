# B0-01 · Corporate source and authority checkpoint

Completed 2026-11-18 by source and contract review. BD10 and [C01–C08](../../prompts/B0-corporations/POLICY-REVIEW.md) supply the policy. This handoff defines proposed contracts for separately selected packages. It introduces no runtime authority.

## Who can act

| Asset owner | Authenticated actor | Permitted in the proposed contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder personally | Connected founder | Register using explicit name and reviewed debit from one selected owned, open personal BANK. A contribution separately authorizes the personal debit and identifies the corporate recipient. |
| Corporation | Fixed founder | Read history, manage accounts, pay, rename, appoint/revoke managers, authorize integrations, start/cancel wind-down and finalize. Offline presence confers no successor rights. |
| Corporation | Appointed player | View/history initially. Owner chooses payment and account-management capabilities independently. Payment permits transfers to self. Management alone cannot direct funds to a different owner. No subdelegation. |
| Corporation | Registered integration | Explicit named organization capability and account scope under a current owner mandate. New registration requires live founder and funding consent. Audit integration identity and any initiating player. |
| Any | Operator or built-in system actor | Existing privileged inspection and internal system paths retain their original targets. Neither supplies corporate spending or management authority. |

**Existing denials.** Journal actor keys round-trip organization UUIDs and PartyRef can represent an organization owner. PartyAuthorization.admit rejects that owner before its player, inspection or package-private internal grant branches. Grant binds session, actor, target and inspection mode. Keep all three factories and generic grant consumers gated. A corporate operation needs its own action-scoped admission. Never put the company in the actor field to simulate delegated authority. PartyAuthorization.ownerKey can encode the owner independently. See [PartyAuthorization](../../src/main/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/foundation/PartyAuthorization.java).

AccountCatalog initialization and owner lookup require a player. BankProviders checks player identity and binds secondary opening to an existing primary revision. AccountLifecycle protects the primary and current denomination. Corporate opening instead checks current organization authority and lifecycle, Tier 1/hybrid provider and native currency, one open account per currency and four open BANK accounts per organization inside the write transaction. Use a distinct typed owner key in catalog constraints, reads and recovery. Never auto-create primary/TRADING, infer a provider from the founder, or strand a corporate account because it holds world currency. Reuse BranchAdmission live kiosk identity, distance, generation, bank, action, revision, lease and expiry checks (at most 30 seconds). Bind the selected organization as well as the player. A funded close must validate the explicit destination, both revisions, claims, holds and any FX quote in the same transaction. Cross-owner payouts require payment authority. See [branch access](../architecture/branch-hardware.md) and [provider lifecycle](../architecture/institutions.md).

## Monetary participation

AccountFunds v2 exposes signed available units plus nonnegative reserved/restricted funds. FundingEligibility is a snapshot check. Recheck available units against the entire reviewed debit under the transaction. Exclude overdraft headroom, tolerance/PAD, credit draw, loans, personal guarantees, wealth qualification, deposit yield and card issuance from all corporate routes, including direct API calls. Retain existing owner authority on the other side of every transfer. Record both owners, actor, currencies, principal, fees and linked postings. See [overdrafts](../architecture/overdrafts.md).

* **Registration.** Debit CAD 500.00 or its equivalent in the selected account currency. Use BankingFees.convert with the reviewed publication for a CAD-denominated fee. CAD rate divided by target rate and converted major amount each truncate at eight places, then round HALF_UP to 25 minor units (25 JPY or 0.25 elsewhere). This is a valuation of a single-currency debit. It does not execute a second FX service. Reject nonpositive, unavailable, stale or overflowing results. Bind rates, publication, arithmetic version and debit to the receipt.
* **Retirement.** PostingBatch.prepareMinor supports a negative ACCOUNT leg and a matching positive ISSUANCE leg under REDEEM. That primitive checks balance/signs. It does not establish who may retire money or resolve a dedicated sink. B0-02 needs a private world/currency registration-sink resolver, restricted redemption handler and compatible replay/projection and activity classification. Debit and retirement, company, name, owner and receipt must share one journal commit. No spendable treasury or generic redemption endpoint.
* **Ordinary banking.** Same-currency movements carry no FX charge. Assigned cross-currency services use Tier 1 even at hybrid providers, 0.50% of source principal rounded upward once to source minor units, additional to principal. Credit bank income through FeeSettlement. Use ordinary settlement rounding for the principal, including whole-yen HALF_UP, and solve principal plus fee for funded FX closure. Add an organization-scoped pricing witness without player valuation or discount. No separate flat open, close or same-currency transfer fee identified. ATM surcharges, advances, replacement-card fees, overdraft fees and trading fees belong to products outside this branch/account path. See [pricing](../architecture/wealth-pricing.md), [precision](../architecture/monetary-precision.md) and [CAD fee implementation](../../src/main/java/ca/emilybelnavis/mcmods/economy/pricing/BankingFees.java).

## Durable state and extension contract

| Proposed identity or projection | Immutable facts and ordering |
| --- | --- |
| Organization UUID in world, founder UUID, lifecycle/revision | Registration with fee/consent, rename, wind-down, cancellation and dissolution. A unique normalized name index retains every alias and tombstone. Apply one versioned Unicode normalization and case-fold to input and reserved bank/system names. Enforce 3–48 code points, allowed punctuation and exclusion of controls/formatting before insertion. |
| World + company + player membership | Appointment, exact independent rights, change/revocation with monotonically increasing revision. |
| World + registered integration + external key | Stable mapping independent of authority. Versioned mandate identifies organization, capabilities, scoped accounts and revocation epoch. Validate server-owned namespace against registered provider, using Journal's ASCII namespaced-key grammar, maximum 128 characters. Reject collisions and undeclared rights. |
| Corporate account + provider + operation | Open/close, binding and posting facts identify typed owner, actual actor and expected account/provider/authority revisions. Retain closed accounts and original history. |

Baseline schema 55, banking wire 13, BankingIntegration v3 and AccountFunds v2. Propose schema 56 for registration, separately versioned corporation facade v1 and v1 corporate facts/requests/results. Use wire 14 when introducing company selection. Recheck availability before allocation. Preserve old decoders and follow the [stopped-world upgrade contract](../architecture/database-upgrades.md).

The facade should return completion stages of immutable capability discovery, company/account snapshots, keyset pages or original operation receipts. Bind cursor to world, organization, actor, filter and revision. Registration accepts founder consent, funding account/revision, name and fee witness. Account/payment commands require server-issued session context, actual integration/player identity, organization, exact action, account scope, canonical operation ID, amounts and expected revisions. Return explicit unavailable, stale, unsupported, insufficient-funds, name-conflict, scope-denied and unknown-commit outcomes without exposing foreign account existence. No SQL, worker callback, packet-provided actor, general grant factory or automatic brokerage authority.

On the server thread authenticate and capture context. On the storage worker resolve equivalent original intent first, conflict on changed intent, then recheck current membership/mandate, organization state and all funding/provider/policy revisions before any fresh effect. Recheck live audience before publication or history disclosure. Disconnect cannot undo a commit. Unknown commit blocks new work until original lookup resolves across reopen. Validate policy changes prospectively and retain effective versions. Compare any CPU plan's inputs again at commit. Never wait for worker completion on the owner thread.

## Bounds and recovery

Default waiting queues allow 256 storage and 64 calculation jobs with a per-actor ceiling of eight. Page indexed company/membership/history lookups at 63 plus one lookahead, independent of total company count. Journal permits 64 request fields/16 KiB, 1–64 records of at most 16 KiB each, 128 KiB total payload and a 16 KiB result. A registration needs two monetary legs and constant-size company/name/authority/receipt data. Budget the serialized records before commit. Check at most four live accounts plus a lookahead for corruption. Wind-down atomically fences opening and integration effects with a new organization epoch. Paginate any cleanup behind that fence. Cancellation never restores the old mandate epoch. Require indexed absence of balances, open accounts, claims, holds and uncertain operations before finalization. If cleanup or evidence exceeds a transaction, stage bounded work under revision and publish only a complete result. See [journal limits](../architecture/journal.md) and [worker admission](../architecture/lifecycle.md).
