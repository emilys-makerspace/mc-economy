# A2-03-02 — FX diagnostics

Privileged detailed diagnostics show the latest detached FX sample, including
selection, reference, committed day, effective and pending policy revisions,
next due day, backlog and waiting or blocked reasons. The existing bounded
worker sampler reads committed facts without advancing the clock. Under
overload the report uses the last sample and shows the retryable reason.

A2-03-03 can use the [FX contract](../architecture/fx.md) and
[verification evidence](verification/A2-03-02.md).
