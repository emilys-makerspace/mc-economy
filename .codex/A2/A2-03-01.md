# A2-03-01 — Daily FX scheduling and configuration

Implemented the trusted opening journal operation, version-1 UTC daily FX
registration, authenticated policy edits and schema-5 lookup indexes.
See the [FX contract](../architecture/fx.md) for the sampler encoding,
effective-policy rules and storage boundaries.

A2-03-02 can read the latest committed publication and its policy UUID on
the storage worker. A1 supplies economic progress and storage failures.
Policy outcomes expose FX_UNINITIALIZED, FX_BOUNDARY_PASSED,
FX_FIXED_REFERENCE and FX_INVALID_POLICY. A2-05 can bind the trusted
opening registration to its server-side chooser. A2-04 can use
`Publication.conversionRates()` for a same-publication conversion input.
