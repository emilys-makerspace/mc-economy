# A2-02 — Conversion and settlement rounding

Implemented and verified on 2026-11-03. See [verification evidence](verification/A2-02.md).

- `CurrencyConversion.Rates` holds a detached map from one publication in
  permanent-reference major units per currency major unit. A supplied
  reference rate must equal one. Callers select the publication.
- Deposits group original cash by currency and floor each converted subtotal.
  Results carry the total credit and the original payable and skipped groups.
  Withdrawals convert requested cash into account currency and ceil the debit.
- Invalid input, missing rates, overflow and zero total credit have distinct
  failure codes. A rejection yields no partial settlement.
- Historical display preserves the sign of sub-minor magnitudes and shows
  exact zero normally. Other historical amounts round half up for display.

A2-03 supplies the publication under the [clock contract](../architecture/recovery-clock.md).
A4 owns posting under the [journal contract](../architecture/journal.md).
A5 owns retaining skipped cash and delivering withdrawals.
