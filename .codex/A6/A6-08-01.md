# A6-08-01 — Funded credit facilities and servicing

Implemented 2026-11-05. Schema V25 adds revolving facilities, separate credit tokens and immutable monthly statements. Issuer-kiosk origination composes the once-only 650 / 2,500 world-currency starter profile with a real cash reservation, token and protected custody delivery. Existing profiles and increases use current underwriting, owner-wide exposure, Tier and finite issuer funding.

Purchase/advance APIs consume the facility reservation and create linked receivable/liability postings. Advances retain the outstanding 20% sublimit. Repayments support owned BANK funding, explicit captured FX/fees, oldest minimum satisfaction and approved bucket allocation. Closure/replacement preserves debt, anchor and history; principal receipts restore reservation only while the line remains open.

Daily anchor-based interest and 28-day statements are installed as two bounded calendar providers. Exact decimal accrual and signed residuals remain separate by bucket. Statements preserve frozen balances/minimums and due = close + 14 financial days. Credit liabilities are a required reconciled net-worth source. Physical credit items use original A6-05 artwork and the existing custody bridge.
