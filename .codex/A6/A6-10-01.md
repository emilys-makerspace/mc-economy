# A6-10-01 — Shared debt lifecycle foundation

Implemented 2026-11-05 in the recorded foundation scope. Cards and loans now track original obligations, delinquency, due+2 reporting and cure revisions, default at oldest unmet due+84days, frozen post-default debt and independent collection/report clocks.

Funded collection sale transfers the claim to Waystone with separate acquisition allowance and proportional recovery income. Unfunded purchases defer while collection remains eligible for the original creditor. Successful positive partial payment resets only the728-day collection window. Settlement retains paid-default visibility until the original2,548-day expiry. Uncollectability ends demands and records loss while retaining historical borrower liability.

Owner LedgerService APIs provide native and explicit-FX protected collection repayment. Revision/expiry checks, separate fees, quote consumption, case updates and original outcomes commit atomically. Ordinary insufficient funds returns a ledger rejection with a usable storage connection.

V31 adds lifecycle projections, five providers and constant-size claim witnesses, replacing the128 shared-lender repayment scan. Institution ALLOWANCE accounts use explicit v2 account facts. Score cadence and new-borrowing activation proceed through the later lifecycle acceptance.

An untracked legacy obligation whose deadline precedes the completed financial frontier requires journal-based reconciliation before startup readiness. Safe bounded bootstrap preserves original future obligations. The rejection boundary and unchanged financial state after recovery are tested.

[Current contracts and APIs](../architecture/debt-lifecycle.md) and [verification](verification/A6-10-01.md) record precise storage, ordering, coverage and limits.
