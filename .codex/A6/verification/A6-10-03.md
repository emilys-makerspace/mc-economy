## Scope and limits

The accepted runtime applies to current and provenance-complete histories. A legacy missed default requiring compensation, or tracked history missing its original obligations, remains an explicit startup reconciliation boundary. The approved policy supplies no method to rewrite already committed later borrowing or interest. Historical missing timestamps/sources/FX are unavailable inputs. Active revolving portfolios are bounded at three, rational precision at8192 bits and canonical task records at16,384 bytes. Input work is paged instead of silently assuming missing rows are zero.

Automatic append-only cure and effective factual-revision consumption are tested. A separate manual correction operation remains unspecified. The same-instant scheduled payment and Tier checks use fixture providers around the real financial/report/score handlers. Interactive player command use and later Collection Agent gameplay are outside this package's recorded acceptance.
