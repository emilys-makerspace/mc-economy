## Reused contracts and remaining limits

A6-02-01 already covers deterministic boundary golden vectors, arithmetic overflow, strict configuration, atomic rollback, uncertain committed-response lookup, additive migration, backup/restore and malformed historical policies. A6-02-02 covers read-only diagnostics, console/operator admission, bounded pages, controlled failures and queue pressure. The full suite retains that evidence without adding duplicate acceptance fixtures.

No persisted entities, decoder versions, mutation/packet APIs, authorization rules or lifecycle authority changed here. Test fixture tables exist only in disposable worlds and are excluded from artifacts. Actual process termination is distinct from earlier exception/response-loss injection; neither establishes hardware power-loss or arbitrary filesystem recovery guarantees.

The shared clock foundation is accepted for downstream adapters. Controlled clocks model elapsed UTC, not a real multi-year wait or a new live Minecraft sleep/pause test. The 225-effect/65-target witnesses establish exercised bounds/order/fairness; they are not a large-portfolio benchmark. Future product adapters still own immutable event-time target membership, paged input folds, domain financial invariants, abuse tests and actual product performance. No debt product, score/report job or physical collection/card effect is installed by these tests.
