# A6-06 — Issued debit cards and lifecycle

Implemented 2026-11-05. Schema V23 adds authoritative issued tokens and one-use replacement fee quotes. Register28 approved debit appearances (18Tier1,10Tier2); each physical item contains only world/token references. No credit facility, borrowing, debt or score is created. The eight legacy generic cards remain unchanged and access-only.

Issuer kiosks offer free first issuance, replacement and renewal. One active debit token per BANK account, five financial years of validity, free renewal within28 financial days before expiry or after expiry. Early replacement costs25 world-major units, captured/converted into the selected owned funding account with ceiling debit and any ordinary FX fee. Remote lost reporting is free. Replacement atomically charges, revokes every old copy, writes the new token and creates an A5 custody obligation. Provider assignment/switch, closure and rebase collision retirement compose revocation.

[Exact contracts and API](../architecture/issued-cards.md) · [verification](verification/A6-06.md).

Authoritative owner/world/issuer/account/provider epoch/status/expiry checks gate item use; copied tokens confer one shared authority, never duplicate benefits. Kiosk proofs and fresh fee/Tier/revision checks gate mutations. Full inventory/disconnect before insertion leaves a protected claim. Proven never-attempted committed delivery survives restart as a claim; ambiguous insertion stays quarantined. SQLite and inventory saves are explicitly non-atomic.

Credit tokens, facilities, advances, scores and applications remain unavailable under their later owners. A6-07 can build profile/underwriting contracts without treating debit issuance as credit establishment; A6-08 must implement distinct facility-linked credit issuance and its first-profile transaction. Neither package was executed.
