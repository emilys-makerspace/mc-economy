# A6-09-01 — Fixed loan foundation

Implemented 2026-11-05. Bank and shared Ironbridge subprime applications use approved score/term/exposure tables, fresh Tier/prime/FX inputs, issuer kiosk authority and finite own-capital funding. Fixed unsecured contracts capture all origination inputs, versioned schedules and immutable accepted offer/denial details. Loans never establish credit or consume a starter entitlement.

Monthly servicing uses APR/13 and 13×years with segmented current-period interest; early payments preserve earned interest, retain the installment and shorten payoff. Repayment and frozen full-payoff quotes support owned BANK funding, explicit FX/fees and audited residual/additional-interest waiver. Explicit foreign disbursement needs no extra BANK slot. Variable and secured requests are rejected; no collateral or margin behavior is installed.

Schema V28 adds loans, projected schedules, immutable monthly installments and application identities. A bounded monthly provider and required exact loan-liability source integrate with credit exposure, valuation and shared receivable diagnostics. Quote/Tier witnesses remain stable under natural time accrual while preserving fresh eligibility, policy and persisted-state checks.

New production loan origination uses the existing lifecycle safety gate. Reads and protected repayment remain available; no application/payment GUI, autopay, default/reporting, collections or score activation is added.
