Replacement requires 24,000 eligible running observations, an active loaded branch, a valid vacant desk and safe standing space. Paused, offline, unavailable and unloaded observations add zero. Progress is checkpointed with at most 200 ticks buffered. Exact reserved UUIDs and original outcomes govern uncertain spawn recovery.
