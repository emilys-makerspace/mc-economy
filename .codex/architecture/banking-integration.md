## Transaction and recovery boundaries

Finance actions use `FinanceMenuSupport` and the existing `BankingAccounts` pending pipeline. Before admission, verified player saving retains the original owner/world, operation/request IDs, kind and canonical fields in `economy_banking_pending`. Its existing cash-operation exclusion applies. Terminal outcomes clear the pointer. An uncertain result retains it for ordered owner-scoped original-outcome lookup. A confirmed absent finance operation requires a fresh reviewed request. A disconnected or replaced player object cannot save over the current login. Menu closure cancels audience publication without reversing a committed financial result.

A held active owned credit token selects credit funding at a register preview. Ordinary account-funded register sales retain their currency restrictions. The optional v1 reserve field `credit_funding=true` allows a credit-funded quote to retain the buyer primary identity/revision when its currency differs. The exact COMMIT canonical variant adds bounded `credit_request`, a sorted representation of the frozen v1 draw fields. Other request extras reject. The ordinary debit composer rejects that variant, preventing fallback to account debit.

At COMMIT, the register checks exact saved stock, price, terminal, source, capacity and evidence. The trusted credit composer binds the original draw, creates a DRAW child intent with the same operation/actor, and validates the sealed current held-card proof and credit readiness. Credit reserve-to-seller posting, borrower liability, facility revision, draw facts, protected goods custody and register state commit in one existing SQLite journal transaction. There is no second financial submission. Original-outcome replay bypasses the composer and does not repeat item delivery. Physical stock removal, observed chunk saving and goods insertion retain the separate A5 custody protocol, known-undelivered claims and ambiguous-effect quarantine.

Approved advances credit a selected owned open same-currency BANK account at an ATM/teller with the held active token. Their draw has its own saved operation and no physical cash side effect. A subsequent separately confirmed A5 withdrawal delivers cash through existing custody. An ambiguous cash delivery cannot repeat the advance. The outstanding-principal 20% sublimit and overall credit ceiling remain authoritative backend checks. Every new advance finances a CAD3.00 base issuer fee plus a CAD2.50 base ATM fee when the ATM bank differs from the card issuer; tellers have no ATM fee. Requested proceeds reach the selected BANK account in full. Both fees enter the advance principal bucket and consume the 20% sublimit, overall headroom and funded reservation. The fee/terminal identity is frozen in the canonical request, checked against current terminal state, and shown with the total debt increase. The issuer receives the advance fee and the ATM bank receives its surcharge through balanced cash/income/borrower-expense postings in the same journal commit. `ADVANCE_FEES` events retain the original breakdown for diagnostics and owner history. CAD bases convert at the captured current FX publication into card currency, rounded once to the nearest 0.25 or 25 JPY (HALF_UP ties). The canonical advance fee witness includes the FX publication ID; changed publications require fresh review.

## Public contract

A8-05 adds authenticated explicit-currency prime(grant,currency) and primeAt(grant,currency,atMillis), returning immutable [currency policies](currency-prime.md) with publication-readiness gating. No currency is inferred when omitted. Owner valuation returns the existing policy, denomination, FX publication and time-stamped `WealthValuation.Snapshot`, including explicit unavailability. Fee quotes retain the existing owner, institution, principal/fee, tier, policy, revision, expiry and valuation witness. Trading quote requests reject while the product is unavailable. Physically authorized advance submission and the atomic register composer retain sealed server proof requirements. No new unrestricted purchase draw is exposed. Capability reporting reuses `Loans.Capabilities`, with fixed unsecured available and variable/secured awaiting their future adapters.

Never-attempted COMMITTED issued-card deliveries now appear in the owner collection page immediately. Startup card recovery validates both debit and credit issuance/replacement provenance before converting a never-attempted delivery to a claim. Attempted or uncertain delivery retains quarantine behavior.
