## Carry and demand transition

A boundary records every scoped unretired legacy carry's original key/epoch and exact residual, including zero residuals, with no deletion, reuse, payout or balance entry. Original carry rows/payloads remain untouched. Boundaries are immutable and validated against their journal and original carry events; projections cannot erase or rewrite history. More than64 carry keys in one boundary refuses the transition.

New reference pricing has no accumulating carry field. Separate `reference_trade_price_events` own only prospective demand updates; initial reads inherit the prior legacy demand/recovery anchor. Source revision changes do not rewrite that anchor. A committed trade updates demand once with its existing stock/progression commit. Legacy price/carry event validators retain their original interpretation. Switching a scope retires legacy fresh admission; original committed legacy operations still resolve before that guard.

`ValuationStore.publish` composes source revisions with the candidate valuation pointer in the same journal transaction for already-enabled scopes only. Each gets the new manifest/input hashes and a deterministic new epoch. Source events use scope-qualified keys so multiple scope rows can share one publication operation/sequence. No inactive scope is created. Refresh is bounded to16 enabled scopes per publication and rejects the entire candidate above that bound. Failed publication commits neither pointer nor source revisions. Historical valuations/quotes remain usable for original replay.

## Rounded-unit pricing and availability

`ReferencePricing` is `economy:reference-unit/v1`, rounding `economy:half-up-unit/v1`. It reuses legacy demand recovery/integrals and reputation bounds. Compute exact execution total, divide by actual goods count, HALF_UP to a positive minor unit (minimum1), then checked multiply that unit by goods count. Quantity-dependent demand still affects the resulting unit. Exact/proof amounts are not spendable fractional money. Foreign service-fee and other financial rounding retain their prior algorithms.

The complete cohort must satisfy a rounded20% gap. The installed proof is conservative: with authenticated effective reputation t, neutral standard ask a/bid b and standard count s, require `.5 * (.8*a*(1-t) - b*(1+t)) / s >= .9` minor units. For all shared reachable demand multipliers m>=.5 and positive quantities, buy integral/q>=m and sell integral/q<=m; HALF_UP errors are at most.5 each. This envelope therefore proves rounded bid<=.8*rounded ask throughout that domain. It can reject states a tighter discrete proof might accept, including ratios at the exact.8 boundary. Such quotes return `ROUNDED_COHORT_SPREAD_UNAVAILABLE`; the code never widens the spread, changes rounding or offers free/partial payment to force availability.

The new service remains an internal foundation. A9-06 owns initial activation, live controller/native exact-state admission and player presentation. No production source selection was changed by A9-05.
