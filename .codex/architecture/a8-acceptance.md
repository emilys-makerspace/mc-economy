## Acceptance boundary

Schema49, Java21, Minecraft1.21.1 and NeoForge21.1.252 remain the delivered platform. The production lifecycle, source/anchor identities, A/F/H, provider implementation and immutable policy/configuration provenance retain the shared contract. Public reads are immutable/cached or bounded asynchronous snapshots; reading does not advance time, seed data or grant money/item authority. Unavailable coverage/pricing/capacity is explicit.

The new composite uses real journal/services/SQLite and the actual macro dispatcher. Its item reservation/save admission is detached fixture evidence; the separate A8-07 client establishes the physical/network path. Reopening SQLite is not abrupt process termination. An entity save does not establish XP-orb persistence. No every-seed/catalog/platform, multiplayer saturation, sustained performance, physical kill/restore permutation or exactly-once cross-store guarantee is claimed. Bounded queues/caches may refuse work, and incomplete cohorts/components remain unsupported until an explicit adapter is supplied.

A8-08 changed no production algorithm, protocol, source fact or schema. The installed temporary source remains immutable EmeraldV1. A9 source switching, source-neutral durable terms and prospective active epochs are future adapter work, not an already supported hot-swap. Historical quotes/trades/balances/activity and captured fixed-loan terms must survive that future change unchanged. See [downstream requirements](a8-downstream.md).
