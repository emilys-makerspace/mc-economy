## Geometry and assets

`BranchKioskBlock.WIDE` is an explicit block-state boolean. Its default is false so old palettes missing the property decode to the original vertical two-cell form: cell0 anchor, cell1 directly above. Loading never expands a legacy kiosk. New item placements explicitly select wide=true; four cells use the existing terminal right vector `facing.getCounterClockWise()` and row-major cell0 lower anchor,1 lower satellite,2 upper anchor column,3 upper satellite. Anchor inversion uses clockwise displacement. ATM/teller direction conventions are unchanged.

`WideTerminalBlock` supplies state-aware cellCount/cellPosition/support/sameGeometry overloads, consumed by placement, anchor resolution, integrity, policy visual refresh and teardown. Support and all occupied cells must be loaded; no chunk loads are forced. New wide kiosks need two sturdy supports and clear unobstructed cells. Collision encloses the console's full two-cell width and Z1..15 depth (rotated with facing). One valid anchor owns the survival drop; creative removal produces none. Satellites do not grant extra drops. Guarded installation/rollback suppresses intermediate integrity teardown. Missing/pending/startup-unready kiosk integrity stays fail-closed instead of guessing that a partial form should be rebuilt or dropped.

Original console models provide the broad screen, counter, institution/currency header, recessed base and side panels. The refined assembly has 57 cuboids with raised keys, card/cash slots, walnut cheeks and rear service hardware, plus 72 original console material textures. There are 24 appearances (neutral+23 banks),96 wide per-cell models/four shared geometry parents,24 inventory models and 24 original horizontal header PNG/SVG pairs. All 48 narrow block models and their raster textures are preserved. Item IDs and bank appearance overrides remain; default inventory art is now wide. [Generator and provenance](../../tools/banking-assets/README.md) documents original CC0-derived project artwork and the `generate_wide_kiosk_assets.py` regeneration command. No downloaded art or specific external integration is added.

## Explicit owner/operator action

The ADMIN menu offers **Upgrade kiosk** for a narrow kiosk, with a free/clear-space/support explanation. Both server action allowlists include the new action. The controller independently rechecks authenticated online owner/operator, same dimension, reach≤6, exact generation/revision, loaded bounds/border, interaction/build permission, both supports, air-only added cells and entity collision. A blocked preflight creates no upgrade intent or physical change; the narrow kiosk remains functional.

The action closes the stale configuration menu and reports its result to the connected actor. No money/material is charged. Generated branch kiosks may be upgraded by an operator, retaining their branch/system identity. An upgrade never rebinds the bank, changes account eligibility, creates accounts/capital or carries placement identity into dropped items.

## V37 journal and physical effect

`journal.KioskUpgrades` defines BEGIN, COMPLETE and ABORT registrations plus `economy:kiosk_geometry` v1 fact. New table `kiosk_geometry_upgrades(world_id,generation,operation_id,expected_revision,phase)` stores PREPARED/COMPLETE/ABORTED and original BEGIN identity. Exact schema and canonical row/historical generation linkage validate on open/recovery/offline inspection. Historical completed upgrades remain valid after a later terminal replacement at the coordinates. Restore compatibility includes 37; worlds 1–36 retain the normal stopped-world backup-before-upgrade requirement. No historical migration/fact decoder is changed.

BEGIN checks exact active kiosk revision, owner/operator/live proof, prior upgrade state and existing unresolved custody guards. It atomically records PREPARED and advances the terminal revision while preserving owner/generation/settings/binding. Fresh finance, ordinary configure and bank rebranding are denied while PREPARED. Existing successful original outcomes remain resolvable; outstanding custody settlement retains its original intent and cannot be overtaken by upgrade admission.

Only a resolved committed BEGIN permits the initiating server-thread session to try one guarded additive world effect. The controller rechecks current actor/dimension/reach/space/support before writing, remaps the original upper cell and installs the extra lower/upper cells with the same anchor block entity. It records a persistent geometry_pending flag and leaves policyReady false. Synchronous known failure rolls back only cells still matching its own writes; it does not delete a new obstruction or replay arbitrary before-images over player changes. SQLite and Minecraft chunk writes are not claimed atomic.

COMPLETE uses an internal operation-scoped detached lease over freshly verified complete wide geometry, same generation and original BEGIN operation/revision. It advances the terminal revision again and records COMPLETE. The anchor then requires normal fresh terminal reconciliation before banking becomes ready. Original BEGIN/COMPLETE replay alone cannot place cells or make a removed/replaced kiosk authoritative. BEGIN reserves enough revision space for completion/abort.

ABORT similarly requires exact complete original narrow geometry with no added matching wide satellites. It records ABORTED, advances revision and restores normal narrow admission without writing physical cells. A later explicit upgrade can use a new original operation after an abort. Pending partial geometry remains PREPARED/disabled and is reported; it is never automatically filled, rolled back after uncertainty or converted into a new placement. An operator may restore either complete footprint, then loaded/use reconciliation can finish or abort. Breaking the valid anchor retains ordinary single-drop/removal semantics and never erases the bank's historical binding.

## Recovery and thread boundary

`terminal.KioskUpgradeController` maintains at most 256 server-owned work entries and starts at most 4 reconciliations per tick. Block-entity load and interaction reobserve existing intent. Server-thread pre-callback validation refreshes/revokes detached actor/geometry flags; storage code reads only those flags, immutable requests and SQL. It retains exact original intent/operation for lookup-first retries after unknown commits or transient admission. Completion uses a separate internal lease because BEGIN invalidates the initiating menu's old revision. No JDBC wait or Minecraft object crosses into the storage worker.

On load, PREPARED+fullwide can COMPLETE, PREPARED+intactnarrow can ABORT, partial/unloaded/uncertain cells remain disabled. Recovery never installs added blocks. A completed SQL record with inconsistent physical form also stays disabled. Removal or server shutdown cannot cause a delayed callback to place hardware. PolicyReady is blocked by geometry_pending, full loaded geometry/support matching and the worker's persisted pending gate; appearance is never authority.

## Downstream template contract

`BranchBootstrap` recognizes captured `narrow` and `wide` geometry; fresh initial activation requires the exact planned form. Existing ACTIVE branch generations can retain an explicit completed upgrade without rewriting their immutable initial capture. A7-04 uses `BranchKioskBlock.wideState(state,cell)` and `TerminalGeometry.cells/anchor`, preserves the original anchor generation and uses `generatedPlacement` before trusted activation. It must not use the no-state legacy two-cell helper to lay out wide kiosks.

The exposed right vector is counterclockwise from FACING (north→west). [Current shared floor-plan table](../A7/architecture/a7-shared-contracts.md) now reserves kiosk anchors at compact local X=2 and larger local X=2/14, giving footprints X=1..2 and 13..14 under north-facing local coordinates. This is a technical floor-plan correction to fit the actual existing terminal direction convention, not a change to approved bank sizes/counts/terrain limits or movement of any existing anchor. Desk geometry reservations are unchanged. No dependent template/generator package is executed here.
