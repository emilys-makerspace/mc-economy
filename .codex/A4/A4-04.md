# A4-04 · Same-currency operations

Verified worker-side primary payments and the shared same-currency operation
registrations. See the [posting contract](../architecture/postings.md) and
[verification](verification/A4-04.md).

The A3 catalog permits one open bank account per owner and currency. As a
result, the owned-transfer handler has no eligible distinct pair in the
current catalog. A4-05 owns cross-currency settlement. A4-06 can bind the
registrations to service entry points and supply live authority.
