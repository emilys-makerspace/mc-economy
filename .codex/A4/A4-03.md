# A4-03 · Holds

Implemented [durable holds](../architecture/postings.md#holds) on the A4-02
settlement boundary and A3 open-bank eligibility. No prerequisite remains
unresolved. See [verification](verification/A4-03.md).

A4-04 can read posted and available funds in one worker snapshot. A4-05
must use Holds.hasActiveClaim alongside its other claim readers before
closure or rebase. Workflow owners use Holds.active to recover outstanding
reservations and supply live authority for transitions.
