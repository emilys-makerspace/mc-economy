# A8-06-02 — Financial-day provider and handover

Implemented the dormant stage-0 macro provider and trusted atomic activation service under V48. Production registers the implementation but does not activate it; A8-07 lifecycle integration remains unselected.

- One event every 1,200,000 ms: 26 stable child targets plus a separate dispatcher completion. Open seals the complete prior day; eleven closes capture at most fourteen daily summaries. The plan child freezes automatic input/output and the selected AUTO/PAUSED/MANUAL basket. Later children publish from that saved basket, including after restart.
- Activation atomically records future A, F=A+day and aligned legacy retirement H, inherited primes/FX anchors, initial immutable control policy and exact installed successor. Coverage must exist from A; the additive v2 window horizon may omit pre-A days without inventing observations or rewriting historical v1 windows. An incomplete or overlapping existing window blocks activation.
- Paused events retain chronology and hold rates. Manual overrides require a completed paused/manual predecessor, complete bounded relative currency vectors and chronological append-only scheduling; invalid absolute/ordered-pair/rounded outcomes are rejected. Manual is one-shot, then holds until another explicit policy. Prospective resume computes current W without deferred moves.
- Additive v2 activation/window/publication facts bind actual macro activation/dispatcher operations. Existing fact versions, fixed completion/publication correspondence and captured loan terms retain their meanings. Readiness remains false until the actual dispatcher completion after the basket child.
